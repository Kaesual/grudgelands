Texture lab

Pick a texture per ground slot, or try an own PNG: 16 px is one node, 32 / 64 px are drawn world-aligned over 2 × 2 / 4 × 4 nodes (Luanti's align_style = "world"). The lab changes the proposal side, the kit and the plant row. “ground” in the header covers all dry land with one ground, to judge repetition; the Top view shows it flat.

How to use

  • Patch: a seeded patch of the zone, today's ground mix and decorations (read from the world generator's rows), or the proposal on top of it. Each proposal item has a toggle, a rough density where one is at stake, a verdict and a note.
  • Kit: the zone's structures in a row, with the block list and what each block drops. Plants: the zone's gathering plants next to the new plants (ruling 11).
  • The small grey key next to each item names it in chat. A stand-in is made of today's textures until its art arrives.
  • Orbit: drag to turn, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Top: drag to pan, wheel to zoom.
