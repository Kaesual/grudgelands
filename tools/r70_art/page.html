04 / Prestige masonry

Five metals.
One family.

Full blocks only. All three directions use the same new, unframed stone ground and the exact colour ramps of the shipped bars. The ornament grows with rank: Iron 4, Steel 5, Silversteel 6, Embersteel 7, Abyssal Steel 8.

Each tile is shown as one cube, its flat face and a 5 × 5 wall with no added grout or random rotations. The strip compares all five tiers with the existing plain stone. Each metal can be picked separately.

About these studies

Compatible, tools only. No game assets, Lua, recipes, world state or hall templates change in this lane. Placement in the six real halls and desktop/web acceptance belong to the installation lane. The existing sorter is beside the west wall: the machines require a revised work bay, not insertion into that occupied strip.
