Today

Shipped construction camp

The current camp, exported from its game blueprint. Wool tents, uneven posts and two timber frames, with no clear construction sequence around the perimeter.

A · Wallwright

The wall goes up

A finished gate and west wall meet an east wall still on its scaffold. Full panels, first courses and pegged footings make a readable sequence around the ring.

B · First Lift

Recommended

A half-height headframe stands behind the gate: one upper portal is up, the other awaits its next beams. A rope hoist and covered shaft collar identify the future mine. The rear wall is still staked out.

C · Panel Yard

An army building in batches

Long canvas barracks flank the parade route. Finished wall panels wait in three racks, next to timber and masonry stacks. Paired survey stakes mark the next wall bays.

The four views share one camera and scale. Select any visible block to inspect it.

A · Follow the wall.

The long scaffold is the focal point. Most of the defence is already standing; the build advances around the ring in distinct bays. This is the most enclosed proposal.

B · Recognize the mine.

The first stage of the headframe gives the strongest distant silhouette and the clearest link to the finished camps. Its shaft is covered, the working deck is open, and its rope carries a timber load.

C · See the workforce.

The low, broad camp is organized around sleeping quarters and a rear assembly yard. Wall panels repeat as complete objects, so the unfinished perimeter reads as planned work.

__OVERVIEW__
All proposals use existing game materials and geometry. No new nodes or textures. Interactive geometry comes directly from the candidate templates; Today comes from the shipped blueprint. Terrain, actors and engine lighting are not simulated. The markers are inspection overlays, not template objects. This file works offline; it contains its renderer, textures, meshes and still overview.
