Where safety ends

Inclusive node centres in arena-local lx, ly, lz. The road surface node is at 0. The wire overlay shows the outer faces of these cells.

The hall roof meets a solid back wall. The gallery’s ceiling covers the stair opening and its roof offers a flat landing above the rail. Both roof surfaces are contested.

The friendly box ends at the wall’s inner face and includes the entire rail. The cyan inspection overlay marks the proposed barrier at . The wall passes daylight and blocks movement, projectiles and targeting for attacks and heals.
