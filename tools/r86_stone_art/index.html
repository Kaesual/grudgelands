Artist’s pick. I’d choose Under One Roof. The lit doorway reads as home at 16 px, and the two gables still identify the account stone after the shutters close.

Each grey cube below is one full node. The daylight studies use the placed draft’s 150/255 opacity. Light levels are 2 for draft, 4 for active and 0 for empty.

Under One Roof

Download study PNG ↓

A lamp in the window is the first thing you look for when you come home. One terracotta gable shelters the personal doorway. Two slate gables share a continuous threshold on the account stone. When the coal runs out, the shutters close.

The Pear Tree

Download study PNG ↓

A garden can carry a family’s history as plainly as a hall. One golden pear hangs above the ochre planter. The account carving forks from a common trunk into two fruiting boughs above blue stone. Without fuel, both trees stand bare.
