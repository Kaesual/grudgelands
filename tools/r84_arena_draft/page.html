Where safety ends

Each hall has room for a dozen players. The plan places the discoverable waystone on an inlaid plinth. An emberglass lamp marks its position in the draft. Two bronze sills mark the edge of friendly territory. Outside each sill an open-air return court bends around a solid screen.

The whole site is protected from building and digging. PvP contact currently lingers for 60 seconds and some area effects cross walls. The territory boundary alone gives no immediate immunity.

At the edge of the works

The example has forest litter to the west and mesa clay to the east. Two broken apple trunks and two basalt outcrops mark the back approaches. The six-node fringe picks up the local soil and a three-node cut or fill. Beyond it, the model shows illustrative terrain.

Across eleven seeds the centre square spans 21–139 nodes of relief. Round 85 still has to fit the terraces, handle lake columns and connect the road where it misses the site.
