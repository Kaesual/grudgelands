Every texture is ARTS's proposal from tools/r51_sea unless marked “exists”. Sand, water, seagrass and the mossy cobble are context. Judge each detail below; the key next to it names it in chat too.

Mast height

What you see

  • Full blocks only: waterlogged planks (two looks), waterlogged logs for keel, stem and ribs, mossy cobble ballast. Water never draws a wall against them.
  • Masts (broken off a few nodes above the deck), rope ends, sail tatters and algae tufts are rooted plants: a full plank with two crossed quads above it, drawn in the water without an air pocket. A mast stands only upright, so a mast of a listing hull stands straight and a fallen one is made of logs.
  • The lowest layers lie in the sand; inside the hull is the water (or sand) the world already had there.
  • Luanti light lights every face as the client does by day without smooth lighting: the light of the node it faces, one level less per node of water (the top water node 14), through Luanti's default light curve. A rooted extension takes the light of the node just above its base, over its whole height, also where it stands in the air. Off: everything evenly lit, to judge the shapes. Below the surface the view gets the water's tint.
  • Quarter turns come for free at placement; the shapes are not turned diagonally.

Orbit: drag to turn, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Swimmer: starts beside the wreck at deck height. Top: drag to pan, wheel to zoom.
