04 / Handoff

Pick one race set and one treatment for each material tier. Each tier is a matching set of eight textures, shared by unlocked and locked chests and the two shelf types. A lock replaces only the front tile.

Candidate files are in ../candidates/. Regenerate with python3 tools/r67_art/generate.py; verify with python3 tools/r67_art/generate.py --check. The page and its images can be moved together and opened without a server.

Final acceptance: after the selected art is integrated, check all six races in Social on desktop and web; inspect each tier from nearby and several metres away; check the lock, lid seam and open interior, and rotate both shelf types.
