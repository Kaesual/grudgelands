The exhaustive one-dimensional bound for the capital step band.
Re-run: luajit measurements/band-bound.lua <slope>

Two adjacent band outputs are decided by 2 * radius + 2 columns of
relief, so for radius = step - 1 every profile over that window with a
bounded per-column move is enumerated. This is a worst case, not a
sample.

Exhaustive 1-D relief sweep, per-column move at most 1.
gentle = worst output step where the relief step is <= 1
excess = worst (output step - relief step) over all pairs
deviation = worst |output - plain terrace|
