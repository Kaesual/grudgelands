The phase concentration survives the fix because it is a consequence
of the shape: the plateaus sit ON the lattice and are flat by
construction, so the only columns that CAN be unclimbable are the band
columns, which are the off-lattice classes. The attribution against the
relief, in relief-attribution.txt, is the tell that actually works.
