The processional line

Seven-wide exterior stair → high arch → low, furnished foyer → paired hall doors with open side aisles → recessed carpet → two broad steps → the monumental throne.

The king stands before the seat, with clear feet and head. His two royal guards stand three nodes to either side. There are no free-standing pillars in the combat area; both sides of the dais stay open.
