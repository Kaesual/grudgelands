- **374 side-wall cells:** install the reviewed joined piers at z=10/16/22/27,

  reattach lamps to the retained piers and remove the four obstructing seats.
  The backs of the piers meet the outer buttresses; no one-cell passage remains
  behind them. Existing service-door points remain in open bays.
- **50 ridge cells:** at x=22, z=8…32, replace the upper slab at y=27 with the
  existing roof full block, and the full block at y=28 with a lower slab. The
  same 1.5-node group moves down 0.5 node.
- **36 gable-edge cells:** at z=8/32 and odd x=5…39, the upper slabs left a
  0.5-node opening above the wall. Replace only those upper slabs with the
  existing roof full block, preserving their top surface. Interior roof slabs
  and every stair course are unchanged. Across six castles this closes 216 gaps.

## Save and checks

`install.py` preserves the six initial sources in `before/`, builds deterministic
candidates in `proposed/`, then uses the real template editor's `Editor.save`:
canonical serialization, shared validator, stale-source hash protection and atomic
write. Every save passed with **zero errors and zero warnings**; complete results
and source/output hashes are in `save-report.json`. The script refuses to overwrite
an unexpected source and can safely recognize its exact previously saved result.

`verify_render.py` reads the actual installed templates with all six actual roster
palettes. Its focused checks verify the four geometry groups, unchanged point
semantics, filled pier backs/open bays, moved lamp supports, preserved roof stairs
and interior slabs, and conservative clear volumes for the king and both throne
guards. Results are in `verification.json`. These are static checks, not a claim
that NPC chase pathfinding was tested.

The five 1280 × 900 PNGs in `renders/` are generated by the real shared building
web renderer from installed Human geometry and the preserved original gable. The
throne and gable close-ups crop surrounding geometry solely for visibility. Both
roof-detail cameras are identical. All final images were visually inspected;
no missing nodes, meshes or textures. `gallery.py` checks each capture's input and
output hashes before embedding it into self-contained `index.html`.
