## Bottle proposals

GPT-6.2 Astra drew the proposals for Grudgelands on a 16 × 16 grid. Each PNG is an original transparent export. The first Healing and Mana options use the second sheet’s body studies. The Vigor chalice, split Stoneskin bottle and Swiftness barrel carry their motifs forward. The pick page embeds the PNGs and the seven existing active-effect emblems.
