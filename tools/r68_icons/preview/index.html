Metals, empty mounts
and stones with character.

Two original 16 × 16 pixel families per group. Start with the native inventory strips, then compare the larger samples. Nothing on this page is installed in the game.

Metal bars

Recommendation: A

The longer ingot silhouette and sloping light fit the shipped tools; B makes the surface marks easier to compare.

A · Cast ingots — Sloping, tapered castings with a different surface cue for each metal.
B · Forged billets — Broad horizontal billets with clipped ends and legible working marks.

Jewellery settings

Recommendation: A

A loose mount can become a ring, amulet or seal; its larger open centre reads more clearly at 16 px.

A · Loose bezels — An empty open socket, with copper or gold trim where the recipe calls for it.
B · Ring blanks — An empty prong crown above a hollow ring shank, ready for a separate stone.

Gems and minerals

Recommendation: A

The six rough silhouettes stay cleaner without host rock; the broad diamond crown clearly marks the top gem.

A · Crystal to jewel — Individual natural specimens become distinct radial cuts, with a broad brilliant diamond.
B · Rock to jewel — Mineral exposures in broken host rock become broad step cuts, with a tall diamond.

Distinctness, with limits

CIE76 ΔE: sRGB → linear RGB → CIE XYZ D65 → Lab; compute the Euclidean distance at each matching pixel and average only where BOTH alpha values are 255. No alignment, resizing, compositing, or background pixels enter the metric. N is the number of shared opaque pixels. Shape differences are deliberately not scored. These numbers are a guide, never a threshold; judge the 16 px strips first.

OLD uses the shipped texture expressions and engine rounding, including the cut-gem brighten modifier. Each scope is measured separately; the gem scope includes all 14 rough, cut and mineral items.
