Three original motif pairs for The Accord and The Throng. Each pair shares a bold, cut-metal construction; the silhouette carries the identity from a small player row to a camp banner.

Recommendation: A · Wing & Tusk

The wide, descending wing and the open, rising jaw are the clearest opposites at 24 px. Both remain distinct as 16 px colour shapes, without depending on tiny interior lines. Blue and red carry the UI; light ink carries the same silhouettes on the cloth.

Read at 100% browser zoom. The size strips use 16, 24, 32 and 64 CSS pixels on #181e28; headers use 54 px and player rows 26 px. All sizes use the same 100 × 100 master. Browser display scaling and game filtering can differ. Class and race pictures are the shipped files.

Faction colour: The Accord #3f6fce · The Throng #c41e3a. UI symbols use two flat colours plus edge antialiasing. Banner ink uses the identical motif outline in warm ivory, on faction cloth with a dark edge and a simple sewn hem.

Banner study: the 128 × 256 textures retain the shipped alpha silhouette exactly. The projections read the existing B3D cloth, rod, straps and UV coordinates, at rest; only the support post and beam are illustrative. One node is treated as one metre; camera distance is approximately 5 m. These are offline projections with a common camera and light, not game captures or a desktop/web acceptance test.

Choice: pick A, B or C as a pair, or name one variant per faction. These are proposals; no game assets have been replaced.
