A hart for The Accord.
A boar for The Throng.

Six new drawings in one heraldic language: deliberate contours, open antler branches, clear animal anatomy. Old ivory on muted cloth, with a fine tarnished-gold shield rim. Choose any hart with any boar.

Recommendation: A1 + B1

The front-facing hart gives The Accord an upright, balanced silhouette; the boar's long profile gives The Throng a forward thrust. Both put the animal's identifying features into the largest possible area of the shield. A2 and B2 offer full-body heraldry; A3 and B3 give alternative head treatments.
