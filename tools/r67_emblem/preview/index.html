Grudgelands / Round 67 / Emblem proposals / Pass 2

Two standards.
One old rivalry.

Three original motif pairs for The Accord and The Throng. Ivory charges on weathered slate-blue and wine-red fields, with tarnished gold borders and small embroidered flourishes. The main silhouette carries the identity; the ornament can fall away at small sizes.

Recommendation: A · Hart & Boar

The branching, front-facing hart contrasts with the low, broad boar in profile. At 24 px the antlers and snout do the work; at 16 px their shapes and muted fields remain distinct. Both feel at home on an old war standard. B deliberately revisits the tree and horn from Pass 1 with organic contours and bound-horn details.

Read at 100% browser zoom. The size strips use 16, 24, 32 and 64 CSS pixels on #181e28; headers use 54 px and player rows 26 px. All sizes use the same 100 × 100 master. Browser display scaling and game filtering can differ. Class and race pictures are the shipped files.

Faction colour: The Accord #344b5a · The Throng #683e39. These cloth bases are sampled from the most frequent fully opaque pixel in each shipped banner. The new UI shields use the same muted fields, old ivory #d6c59b and tarnished gold #aa9164. Three material tones carry the image; dye, thread and antialiasing add subtle variations. Banner cloth has a fine weave, faded edges and a rubbed hem; the original alpha cut is unchanged.

Banner study: the 128 × 256 textures retain the shipped alpha silhouette exactly. The projections read the existing B3D cloth, rod, straps and UV coordinates, at rest; only the support post and beam are illustrative. One node is treated as one metre; camera distance is approximately 5 m. These are offline projections with a common camera and light, not game captures or a desktop/web acceptance test.

Choice: pick A, B or C as a pair, or name one variant per faction. These are proposals; no game assets have been replaced.
