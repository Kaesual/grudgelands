# Under One Roof

Original 16 × 16 pixel art for Grudgelands by GPT-6.2 Astra, dedicated to
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each PNG is
a byte-identical copy of its Round 86 study master with a game filename.
The two active front tiles also name the stone in its waypoint row
(`grug_housing.STONE_TEXTURE`); the map draws its own claim icon there. Each outlined item
sprite serves both the inventory and the hand.
