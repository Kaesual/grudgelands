## Own pixel art — CC0 1.0

Generated deterministically by **`tools/r33_c3/gen_cloak_textures.py`**
(seed 20261004) from the palettes in `tools/r33_c3/cloak_palettes.json`
(GPT-6 Astra's cloak proposals); a re-run reproduces every PNG byte for byte.
Placeholders: cloth in the base colour, the lining in the shadow colour, a
plain emblem block and one hem stripe per tier. Astra's painted cloaks of the
same names replace them.
