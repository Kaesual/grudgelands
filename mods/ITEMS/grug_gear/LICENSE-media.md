# Media Origin & Licenses (grug_gear)

Two licences live here, and the split is by origin, not by taste: the **armor**
icons are this project's own CC0 art, and the **weapon** icons are derivatives
of minetest_game's CC BY-SA 3.0 tool sprites.

## Weapon icons — CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported

WP13's playtest round 2 replaced the four vertically drawn weapon sprites
(`grug_gear_item_sword.png` and friends, one per family, tinted per bracket)
with 25 sprites in **minetest_game's diagonal tool convention** — grip
bottom-left, business end top-right — one per family *and* material, because
the items are material-named now (`items_crafting.md` §3.0.3) and a Bronze
Sword should be bronze rather than a grey sword multiplied by a bracket colour.
One convention for every held item in the game is also what lets
`mods/PLAYER/grug_visuals/wield_geometry.lua` derive a single hand transform.

All 25 are generated by **`tools/wp13/gen_weapon_ladder.py`**, deterministically
(re-running it reproduces every byte), from two sources:

- **the sword shape** is `mods/BASE/default/textures/default_tool_steelsword.png`
  with its six grey steps mapped through a per-material lookup and its wooden
  handle copied unchanged. That is upstream's own recolouring method: the
  generator's self-check reproduces `default_tool_bronzesword.png` pixel for
  pixel, and fails if it ever stops doing so;
- **the dagger, greataxe and staff shapes** are authored in the generator as
  ASCII maps in the same convention and painted from the same palette.

Both are derivative works of minetest_game's tool art, so the whole set is
**CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported** (<http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>).
Original © 2010–2023 the minetest_game contributors listed in
`mods/BASE/default/license.txt` (minetest_game does not attribute these media
per file); vendored at the commit recorded in [VENDOR.md](../../../VENDOR.md).

## Own pixel art — CC0 1.0

Original 16×16 art of this project, **not** derived from any vendored or
third-party asset. Authored as ASCII maps plus a palette in
`tools/gen_mob_item_textures.py` (list `GEAR_ICONS`) and generated
deterministically — re-running the script reproduces every PNG byte for byte.

**License: CC0 1.0 Universal** (<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>).

Four base arts remain — the four metal armor slots. (The four weapon arts this
table used to list were retired in WP13's playtest round 2; see the CC BY-SA
section above.) The cloth line **reuses the metal arts with a cloth palette**
(same trick as `FEATHER`/`BOLT` in the mob icons), and the six tiers are
differentiated at runtime by a `^[multiply:<tint>` modifier — one PNG per slot
and line, not one per tier.
