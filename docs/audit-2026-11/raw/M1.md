# M1 — Mapgen wp40: terrain, zones, map adapter

- Finder model: claude-opus-5-5
- Files read in full: 11 of 12; skimmed: `wp40/terrain_data.lua` (data tables: landmarks, ranges, water knobs; usage checked by `rg`), parts of `wp40/source/simple_map.lua` (anchor and zone rows read row by row, not cross-checked one by one); not reached: none
- Summary: The package is in good shape for determinism: no `x ^ 2`, no `math.random`, every `pairs` that feeds output is either sorted afterwards or per-key independent, and the only clock read (`zone_field.lua` `os.clock`) feeds a log line. `simple_map.lua` and `source/simple_map.lua` are not duplicates: the first is the horizontal-session mechanism, the second the authored data table both environments load. The most valuable items are (1) a per-column cost on the cold planner path: `deterministic.floor_div` (two full integer validations plus a divmod loop) is used for plain 80/128-node bucket indexing about 40 times per cold column tuple in `height.lua` and `zones.lua`, (2) a large amount of dead code kept alive by presence checks (about 550 of 887 lines of `index128.lua`, most of `deterministic.lua`'s Q16.16 and value-noise part, a dozen horizontal-session methods, the `grug_core.zone_at`/`territory_at` adapters, and per-column stubs that always return nil), and (3) two technical/design docs that describe code that no longer exists or behaves differently (`mapgen.md` "The difficulty seam", `world_zones.md` §13.3 shelf/channel levels). `map_adapter.lua` is the R5 VoxelManip adapter of the chunk writer, not the in-game map; its per-chunk waste is small. Upgrade trap: every file of this package except `map_adapter.lua` and `counting_allocator.lua` is in the preparation-authority digest (`preparation_identity.lua`), so even a byte-identical-output edit makes a world in `full` preparation mode assert at its next start (`grug_core/starts_preload.lua:111`); proposals below say this per item.

## Findings

### M1-01 | Medium | perf | Validated `deterministic.floor_div` used for plain bucket indexing on the per-column path

- **Where:** `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/height.lua:136`, `:198-213` (`add_bucket`, `bucket_at`), `:623-650` (`block_for`); `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/zones.lua:845-846` (`logical_biome_at`); also `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/index128.lua:615-626` (`footprint_candidates`, called by `zones.lua:996/1009/1024` per column) and `zones.lua:294-311` (`normalize_xz`, run on every `planner_source.column_values_at` call including cache hits, `zones.lua:1576-1592`)
- **What:** `deterministic.floor_div` (`deterministic.lua:71-78`) runs `integer()` on dividend and divisor (seven checks each) and `divmod_nonnegative` (abs, floor, two correction loops) for every call. `height.lua` uses it for the memo-block key (`block_for`, two calls per `column()`) and for every fitting/authored/square bucket lookup (`bucket_at`, two calls each). One cold planner column tuple (`zones.lua:1495-1574`) reaches `column()` through `inland_water_at`, `terrain_height_at` → `final_values_at` → `land_values_at` → `natural_height_at`, `water_surface_at`, `functional_surface_values_at`, the four shore neighbours (`exposed_shore_at`, `height.lua:1506-1528`) and `bucket_at` through `fit_land` (three grids), `in_capital_core`, `square_at`, `core_excess` — on the order of 40 `floor_div` calls per tuple, plus two in `logical_biome_at`, plus the validations of `index128.footprint_candidates` in `hard_row_at`. The inputs are always integers in range (an out-of-range or fractional value would already throw today), so `math.floor(v / d)` returns the same value.
- **Evidence:** callers above; the cold planner (column tuples) is the largest surface-chunk cost after Round 49: "The cold planner remains the largest surface cost: 93–102 ms of A_S1" and "a new 80 x 80 chunk column with its halo misses about 7,200 tuples (about 6 us each)" (`docs/research/mapgen-chunk-profile-2026-10.md` after-table, `zones.lua:1448-1451`). The tuple is computed for every first chunk of a column on every emerge thread.
- **Proposal:** in `height.lua` use a local `floor((v + BLOCK_OFFSET) / BLOCK)` in `block_for` and `floor(v / FEATURE_CELL)` in `add_bucket`/`bucket_at` (128 is a power of two, 80 divides exactly enough: for |v| < 2^40 the float quotient never rounds across an integer); the same in `zones.lua:845-846` for `cell_size` 192. In `index128.footprint_candidates` drop `assert_sparse_kind` and the two `integer()` calls on the hot path (keep them in `compile_footprints`), or have `zones.lua` hold the compiled `cells` and index them directly. In `planner_source.column_values_at` take a fast path when `x % 1 == 0 and z % 1 == 0` (then `normalize_coordinate` returns its input) before the full normalisation. Generation output stays byte-identical (same integers everywhere).
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible for the output; but `height.lua`, `zones.lua`, `index128.lua` are in the preparation authority, so a `full`-mode world asserts on its next start — ship with the next map reset or after relaxing that guard
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before/after with the `tools/mgprof` stage boots of `docs/research/mapgen-chunk-profile-2026-10.md` (same seed, same corpus chunks): stages "planner R5" and "planner R6 columns" of A_S1 and B_B (cold first chunk of a column), and the warm A_S2 for the hit path; plus `tools/r49_cache` to prove hit equals miss.
- **Confidence:** high (equivalence and call counts); medium (size of the gain)
