# X4 — Server tick at 100 players

- Finder model: claude-opus-5-5
- Files read in full: 0 of 0 (cross-cutting package, no file list); read at every globalstep, ABM, LBM, node-timer, entity `on_step`/`do_custom` and recurring `core.after` site found by `rg` over `mods/` (about 60 globalstep bodies, the mob step in `mods/ENTITIES/mobs/api.lua:3985-4103` with the functions it calls, the grug_mobs `do_custom` wrapper and its per-step helpers); skimmed: the vendored mobs_redo bodies outside GRUG PATCH sites (`general_attack`, `do_states`, `falling`, `get_nodes`, `mob_expire`), `grug_mobs/verbs.lua` chains, the renewal and spawn-region internals; not reached: per-step helpers of the bespoke actors (`bosses.lua` king/general ticks, `boss_dragons.lua` whelp/dragon ticks, `kraken.lua`, `start_villagers.lua` resident/elder ticks) beyond their entry lines.
- Summary: Almost every recurring site is throttled, slot-spread or event-gated, and HUD writers are change-gated; the Round 29 perf review (docs/research/perf-review-2026-10.md) and its Round 30 fixes hold. The cost that grows with 100 players is concentrated in three places: the contextual-input pass that runs every server step for every player and does a privilege lookup plus up to three wielded-item copies each time (X4-01, X4-02), the tag-carrier pass that is carriers x players every second (X4-03), and per-attacking-mob line-of-sight rays every server step (X4-04). A structural item is that about ten "all players in one step" passes share the same phase and fire on the same server step (X4-05). The rule "no pass handles every player or zone in one step" is broken by 20 sites (listed in the inventory), most of them cheap; the ones that do real work per player are ranked below. Nothing found is unbounded: no unthrottled heavy globalstep, no particle spawner without a bound, no per-tick inventory write.

## Findings

### X4-01 | High | perf | Privilege lookup for every player on every server step (contextual input), more in crosshair and environment damage

- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/input.lua:68-74` (`allowed`), called unconditionally at `input.lua:579` from `step`, which `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/init.lua:2957-2974` runs for every connected player every pass; `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/crosshair.lua:157` (`input.allowed` again, every 0.15 s); `mods/CORE/grug_core/environment_damage.lua:167-168` (`core.get_player_privs` for every player every second).
- **What:** `allowed()` calls `core.check_player_privs(player, {interact = true})` (a fresh table plus the auth handler) on every input pass for every player. The pass threshold is 0.05 s, so it runs on every server step (the comment at `init.lua:2981-2983` notes steps of about 0.09 s): about 11 lookups per player per second, 1,100/s at 100 players, plus up to 670/s from the crosshair. Environment damage fetches the full privilege table for every player every second although it only needs `noclip` when the head node is solid (`def` is non-nil for air too).
- **Evidence:** the project's own measurement: `check_player_privs` costs "2.7–6 µs isolated, 35 µs average under combat load" (perf review #17, `docs/research/perf-review-2026-10.md:389`), which is why `mods/ENTITIES/mobs/api.lua:50-75` caches answers per player and privilege (`mobs.has_priv`, invalidated by the grant/revoke callbacks). The input path never got that cache: `rg -n "check_player_privs|get_player_privs" mods` shows `input.lua:69` and `environment_damage.lua:168` as the only per-tick callers. Estimate at 100 players: 1,800 lookups/s, 5–11 ms/s isolated and up to about 60 ms/s under load. Engine auth behaviour not checked (reference_projects/luanti is not checked out).
- **Proposal:** move `mobs.has_priv` (cache plus `register_on_priv_grant/revoke` and leave invalidation) to grug_core as `grug_core.has_priv(name, priv)` and use it in `input.lua:69`, in `mobs/api.lua`, and in environment damage only after `should_suffocate` needs it (look up `noclip` lazily when `def.walkable`). Answers must stay identical, including a privilege change mid-session (the callbacks already cover `/grant`, `/revoke` and `core.set_player_privs`).
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before/after with 100 stand-in players idling (seed 12345, a start town): Lua time of the `grug_abilities/init.lua:2957` globalstep per second (profiler or `core.get_us_time` around `M.step`), and step p95 from grug_perf minute lines.
- **Confidence:** high

### X4-02 | Medium | perf | The wielded item is copied about 50 times a second per player by five separate pollers

- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/input.lua:575` (`get_wielded_item():get_name()` every pass) and `input.lua:75-77` → `init.lua:3138,3147` (`api.selected`, a second copy in the same pass); `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/init.lua:1547-1551` (`selected_swing_def`, a third copy in the same pass, `init.lua:2970`); `mods/PLAYER/grug_map/minimap.lua:522-526` (`claim_mode`, every server step for every player, `minimap.lua:931-953`); `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/crosshair.lua:139` (`api.selected`, every 0.15 s); `mods/PLAYER/grug_visuals/apply.lua:721-737` (1 s); `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/init.lua:612-640` (`watch_wield`, which already keeps `{index, item}` per player).
- **What:** `get_wielded_item()` returns a new ItemStack (userdata plus a copy of the stack and its metadata). Per player and server step the input pass makes three copies and the minimap a fourth; crosshair and visuals add more: roughly 4 x 11 + 7 + 1 ≈ 52 copies per player per second, about 5,000/s and the matching garbage at 100 players. The comment at `init.lua:2944-2946` ("Do not copy the equipped ItemStack in this 20 Hz HUD check") states the intent, but `selected_swing_def` called by the same pass copies the wielded stack anyway.
- **Evidence:** the lines above; `rg -c get_wielded_item mods` gives 44 sites, these six run on a timer.
- **Proposal:** one per-step wield cache in grug_abilities (or grug_core): name and index per player, filled once per server step by the input pass (it already reads both at `input.lua:575`), invalidated by `register_on_player_inventory_action`, `set_wielded_item` wrappers and the hotbar switch (`H.rescan` already marks those events). `api.selected`, `selected_swing_def`, `claim_mode` and the visuals poll read the cached name. What must stay identical: the same-step item switch detection in `input.lua:577-590` (keep reading the live stack there, the cache is filled from that read).
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** medium (wield changes by Lua writes must invalidate) · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Count `get_wielded_item` calls per second with a wrapper and Lua time of the input and minimap globalsteps, 100 stand-ins, before/after.
- **Confidence:** high

### X4-03 | Medium | perf | Tag-carrier pass is carriers x players every second, with a fresh table per carrier

- **Where:** `mods/CORE/grug_core/tag_carrier.lua:488-544` (`update_observers`), `:595-607` (the 8-slot globalstep), `:341-360` (`refresh_snapshot`), `:291-339` and `:266-273` (HP bar, `get_properties()` of the parent per visible injured mob per second); consumer `mods/PLAYER/grug_quests/npc.lua:419-445` (called each second for every carrier with viewers).
- **What:** Slotting (Round 30) removed the one-step spike, but each carrier still walks the whole player snapshot once a second and allocates a new `observers` table (and `both` when a foe carrier exists). Every mob, settlement NPC and player has a carrier (plus a foe carrier near the other side), so at the 600-mob active cap plus NPCs and 100 players this is on the order of 100,000 distance tests and about 1,000 table allocations per second. `refresh_snapshot` also runs on every slot step (8/s) and reads every player's faction from player meta (`grug_factions/init.lua:53-58`). The quest-marker callback calls `child:set_observers(...)` every second for each marker, changed or not (`npc.lua:443`).
- **Evidence:** perf review #10 measured 69 µs per pass with 13 carriers and 0 players (`perf-review-2026-10.md` Appendix A); cost is linear in both factors. `mob_active_limit = 600` (`minetest.conf:47`); authored NPCs are not counted against it.
- **Proposal:** (a) bucket the snapshot by 32-node XZ cell once per snapshot and test only players in the carrier's and neighbouring cells (SHOW/HIDE radii are 25/30); (b) reuse the observer table when the set is unchanged (compare while building, allocate only on change); (c) refresh the snapshot once per second (as SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL says) instead of on every slot step, and cache the faction per player (invalidate in `set_faction`); (d) in `npc.lua`, call `set_observers` only when the partition changed. The hysteresis and the friend/foe split must stay identical.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured at scale. Before/after: Lua time of the tag-carrier globalstep per second with 100 stand-in players spread over 3 towns and 600 mobs (the perf review's helper C probe).
- **Confidence:** medium

### X4-04 | Medium | perf | Every attacking mob casts a line-of-sight ray on every server step

- **Where:** `mods/ENTITIES/mobs/api.lua:4079` (`do_states(dtime)` every step while `state == "attack"`), `:2447-2457` (GRUG PATCH: fresh target snapshot and `grug_obstacle.target_visible`), `mods/ENTITIES/mobs/grug_obstacle.lua:342-358` (`self:line_of_sight` = `core.raycast`, `api.lua:687-689`); also per step `grug_mobs` `do_custom` reads the target's faction from player meta (`mods/ENTITIES/grug_mobs/init.lua:981-987`).
- **What:** The attack branch is the one mob path that runs every step rather than at 1 Hz, and it pays a raycast each time (4.5 µs over 16 nodes, perf review §4) plus the rest of `do_states` (25–36 µs including the ray, measured with 20 guards vs 40 hostiles). With 100 players fighting, a few hundred mobs can be in attack state at once; at about 11 steps/s that is the largest per-mob cost class (for example 300 mobs x 11 x 30 µs ≈ 100 ms/s).
- **Evidence:** the lines above; the measurement in `perf-review-2026-10.md` §4 "Mobs" was at 60 mobs, not at 100-player scale.
- **Proposal:** keep `in_sight` per mob for a short time (for example 0.2 s, or until mob or target moved more than 0.5 node), reusing it for navigation and the strike check as the patch already does within one step; read the attacker's faction from a per-player cache (see X4-03 c). This changes when a mob notices a broken line of sight by up to the cache age, so it needs a ruling on acceptable latency.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** medium · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured at scale. The helper A probe (`helpers/A/grug_probe_perf_a`) with 100 stand-ins and 300 hostiles engaged: Lua time per step and p99, before/after.
- **Confidence:** medium

### X4-05 | Medium | structure | About ten all-player passes share one phase and fire on the same server step

- **Where:** 1 s passes reset with `elapsed = 0`: `mods/PLAYER/grug_pvp/init.lua:305-312` (zone queries for every player), `mods/PLAYER/grug_home/waypoints.lua:517-531` (meta decode for every player), `mods/PLAYER/grug_visuals/apply.lua:721-738` (wield poll), `mods/PLAYER/grug_jobs/workspaces.lua:302-316`, `mods/ENTITIES/grug_mobs/rift.lua:445-449`, `mods/PLAYER/grug_parties/init.lua:436-440`; 0.5 s passes reset the same way: `mods/ENTITIES/grug_mobs/target_frame.lua:223-235` (a raycast and possibly `get_properties()` per player), `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/init.lua:2986-3027` (regen, `watch_wield`, HUD bars), `mods/ITEMS/grug_farming/init.lua:672-678`; the `% 1` cohort: `mods/CORE/grug_core/atmosphere.lua:256-267`, `mods/PLAYER/grug_pvp/page.lua:93-101`, `mods/PLAYER/grug_inventory/pages.lua:599-617`, `mods/CORE/grug_core/environment_damage.lua:153-196`, `mods/CORE/grug_core/status.lua:464-474` (0.5 s).
- **What:** All accumulators start at 0 at load and receive the same dtime, so passes with the same period and reset style fire on the same step forever, and the 0.5 s ones coincide with the 1 s ones every second firing. Each of them loops over every player in one step (the AGENTS.md rule "No pass handles every player or zone in one step"), so once a second one server step carries all of them together: about 100 raycasts, 100 zone queries, 100 meta decodes, 100 wield copies, 100 HUD-bar passes and the 1 s mob-independent work.
- **Evidence:** the reset lines above (`rg -n "elapsed = 0$|% 1$" mods`); the slot pattern used elsewhere (`atmosphere_zones.lua:600-641`, `grug_parties/hud.lua:224-237`, `grug_quests/hud.lua:279-318`, `grug_jobs/basic_unlock.lua:399-412`) is the project's own fix.
- **Proposal:** move the passes that do per-player work (target_frame, pvp sample, waypoints, environment damage, status, visuals wield poll, abilities regen, pvp page, pages) onto the join-slot pattern (8 slots per period, `slot = join_counter % SLOTS + 1`), ideally through one small shared helper in grug_core so the pattern is not written a twelfth time. Per-player periods stay the same.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. grug_perf spike lines and step p95/max over 10 minutes with 100 stand-ins, before/after.
- **Confidence:** medium

### X4-06 | Medium | perf | The spawn density check sweeps all objects within 128 nodes on every spawn attempt that reaches it

- **Where:** `mods/ENTITIES/grug_mobs/density.lua:101-126` (`core.get_objects_inside_radius(pos, count_radius)`, `count_radius = active_block_range x 16 x 2` = 128 at the default 4), called from `SR.attempt` (`mods/ENTITIES/grug_mobs/spawn_regions.lua:932-935`), one attempt per player per second (`spawn_regions.lua:1302-1340`).
- **What:** Every attempt that passes the cheaper gates calls `get_luaentity()` on every object in a 128-node sphere: mobs, but also their tag carriers, foe carriers, HP bars, quest markers, item drops, arrows and other players' objects. Where players gather (towns, events) the density gate is the one that refuses, so nearly every attempt pays the full sweep; at 100 players that is up to 100 sweeps/s over a population that also grows with players.
- **Evidence:** perf review §4 measured `region_density_allows` at 15–33 µs, with the probe's small object counts; `get_objects_inside_radius` costs about 0.05 µs per object plus a `get_luaentity` per object. With 2,000 objects in range this becomes several hundred µs per attempt.
- **Proposal:** count only from the mob registry instead of a sphere query: grug_mobs already tracks active mobs per lifecycle (`api.lua` active-mob accounting, GRUG PATCH at `api.lua:113`); keep a per-zone (or per 64-node cell) count of `_grug_area`/`_grug_spawn_clock` mobs updated on activate/deactivate, and keep the radius rule only as a fallback. Alternatively cache the answer per zone and clock for one second. Decisions must stay the same for the same population.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** medium · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured at scale. Time `region_density_allows` with 100 stand-ins in one capital and 600 mobs plus carriers loaded, before/after.
- **Confidence:** medium

### X4-07 | Medium | perf | Minimap update runs for every player on every server step

- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_map/minimap.lua:931-953` (unthrottled globalstep), `:686-760` (`update`), `:642-682` (`draw_party`).
- **What:** The smooth glide is the reason for per-step updates, and Round 30 hoisted window reads to 0.5 s, but the per-step path still calls `claim_mode` (a wielded-item copy, X4-02), `flight_mode`, `locate` (a `get_pos`), housing's `claim_set_version`, `party_names` and `draw_party` for every player every step. Perf review #12 measured about 45 µs per player-step before the hoist (about 20 ms/s at 40 players); the current cost is not re-measured and scales to roughly 50 ms/s at 100 players if the hoist saved little.
- **Evidence:** `minimap.lua:931-953`; perf review #12 (`perf-review-2026-10.md:366-379`); grug_perf already exports `minimap_us` per minute (`mods/CORE/grug_perf/init.lua:101-107`).
- **Proposal:** first read `minimap_us` from a 100-stand-in run. If it is material: skip `update` when the player's position, yaw and the dirty flags are unchanged since the last step (a stood-still player is the common case), and read the wield name and flight state from per-step caches.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** grug_perf `minimap_us` per minute with 100 stand-ins (half moving, half idle), before/after.
- **Confidence:** medium

### X4-09 | Low | perf | Environment damage builds every player's full property table every second

- **Where:** `mods/CORE/grug_core/environment_damage.lua:160-196` (`player:get_properties()` per living player per second, in one step).
- **What:** It needs only `collisionbox`, `eye_height` and `hp_max`; `get_properties()` builds the whole property table (textures, colors, nametag fields) each time. Together with the eager privilege table (X4-01) this is the pass's whole cost.

- **Proposal:** cache the three fields per player, refreshed where they change (grug_core's box/eye writes, `hp_max` writes) or once every 10 s; spread the pass over slots (X4-05).
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Lua time of this globalstep with 100 stand-ins, before/after.
- **Confidence:** high
