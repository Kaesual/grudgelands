- Summary: The four mods are in good shape on the lifecycle questions the package was given. Expedition runs are memory-only and every exit path (end, expel, logout, restart, death, slot reuse) has a rule that ends somewhere; no stranded-player path was found. Housing state is cleared by the map reset, and grug_home's claim target falls back on its own. The engine submodule `reference_projects/luanti` is empty in this checkout, so every engine claim below is marked "engine behaviour not checked".

## Findings

### P4-01 | Medium | edge | Flyer set-down at login teleports the player back to the logout column after a teleport or respawn

- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_mounts/restore.lua:98-151` (also `:153-175`, `:177-193`)
- **What:** `set_down` stores `pos` once and later calls `p:set_pos({x = pos.x, y = ground.y, z = pos.z})`, or `p:set_pos(at)` for a neighbour column, from an emerge callback or a 1 s retry, up to 30 s later. It never checks whether the player has moved meanwhile. `/home` works during the hold (`home_command.lua` checks creation, home, pending and hp only), and so does a death followed by a quick respawn. Either one moves the player through `grug_home/travel.lua`, and the next `look` then teleports them back to the ground under the old logout position. The token is not dropped on death: `pending` is cleared only by `release`, join and leave, and `look` releases only when it runs while `hp <= 0`.
- **Evidence:** `restore.lua:100` `local pos = player:get_pos()`. `:119-126`: `look` checks only `pending[name] ~= token` and `hp <= 0` before `p:set_pos({x = pos.x, y = ground.y, z = pos.z})`. `:129-133` does the same for the neighbour columns. The file has no `register_on_dieplayer`. A refused restore is a common case: `attempt` sends every flyer whose block is not active within `RESTORE_WAIT = 5` s to `set_down` (`:164-173`), and that happens easily high over ungenerated air. Meanwhile the player hangs held in mid-air and will naturally type `/home`.
- **Proposal:** In `look`, release without moving when the player's horizontal distance to `pos` is more than about 1 node, or when the player is attached. Add `core.register_on_dieplayer` that drops `pending[name]` and releases the hold, as `release` does. A set-down that runs undisturbed behaves exactly as before.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** high

### P4-02 | Medium | perf | Claim Stone check walks, copies and sorts every claim every 5 s in one step

- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_housing/stone.lua:230-260`; `mods/PLAYER/grug_housing/registry.lua:284-289` (all_claims), `:699-709` (draft_scan), `:745-756` (expiry_scan)
- **What:** One globalstep runs three full passes over all claims every 5 s. Claims persist for offline owners for up to 30 days of fuel, so on a long-lived server their number is not bounded by players online. The three passes are `draft_scan` (pairs over every claim plus a sort), `expiry_scan` (pairs over every claim plus a sort) and `all_claims` (a new array and a sort). Each claim then costs one `core.get_node_or_nil`, and a stone node costs a second one in `sync_stone_node` (`api.lua:226-238`). This breaks AGENTS "Performance": "No pass handles every player or zone in one step".
- **Evidence:** `stone.lua:236` `model.draft_scan()`, `:238` `model.expiry_scan()`, `:243-259` `model.all_claims()` plus `core.get_node_or_nil(claim.center)` and `grug_housing.sync_stone_node(claim)` (which reads the node again). Drafts are rare (5 min each) and expiries happen once per `paid_until`, yet both scans run in full every time.
- **Proposal:** Keep a draft set in the registry (add in `create`, drop in `activate` and `remove`) so `draft_scan` touches only drafts. Keep the next expiry time (minimum `paid_until` over claims not yet reported) so `expiry_scan` returns at once until then. Spread the node-sync pass over slots (for example a fixed slice of the id-ordered list per 5 s step), or pass the node already read into `sync_stone_node`. Draft expiry and expiry events, their order by id, and the vanished-stone rule must stay identical.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before and after: a test world with 1,000 synthetic claims (registry stand-ins in mod storage) and one player, timing the globalstep (os.clock around the body) over 60 s.
- **Confidence:** high

### P4-03 | Medium | perf | Waystone discovery reads and decodes every player's meta once a second, all players in one step

- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_home/waypoints.lua:514-531` (with `:64-66` known_set and `waypoints_core.lua:32-59`)
- **What:** Once a second, for every connected player, the pass reads `player:get_meta():get_string("grug_home:waypoints")` and decodes it into a new set (a gmatch over the ids). It then builds a new network list per player (`rules.network`) and allocates a result list, all to test 7 stones against the player's position. The stored set changes only in `discover`, so almost all of this repeats. It is also one step for all players (AGENTS "Performance").
- **Evidence:** `waypoints.lua:521-529`: `for _, player in ipairs(core.get_connected_players())` → `known_set(player)` → `rules.decode(player:get_meta():get_string(KEY))`; `rules.newly_found` → `M.network(rows, faction)` allocates per call (`waypoints_core.lua:32-38`). For 100 players that is 100 meta reads and roughly 300 table allocations a second.
- **Proposal:** Cache the decoded set per player name: fill it at join (or first use), update it in `discover`, drop it at leave. Precompute the two faction networks once at load. Optionally skip a player whose own network's stones are all known: cache an "all known" flag. Discovery timing and the stored text stay identical.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before and after: 100 fake players (the r29_w probe's stand-ins), the globalstep's time over 60 s.
- **Confidence:** high

### P4-04 | Low | perf | Flying mounts ask the zone authority every step and 112 columns a second per rider

- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_mounts/entity.lua:712-723` (per step), `:353-363` (warning sweep), `mods/PLAYER/grug_mounts/flight.lua:197-200`
- **What:** On every server step, each flying rider calls `grug_mounts.flight_state` → `column_flight_state` → `grug_zones.water_class_at` and `grug_zones.id_at`. Each of those normalises the coordinates and runs `classification_values` (`mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/zones.lua:1155-1160`, `:1265-1276`, the latter also `height.inland_water_at`). In addition, the once-a-second warning sweep evaluates up to 16 × 7 = 112 columns per rider, each probe doing the same two or three classifications. The enemy-town check beside it is already cached per node (`:703-711`). The flight check is not, although its answer depends only on faction and column.
- **Evidence:** `entity.lua:713` `local legal = grug_mounts.flight_state(player, pos)` runs unconditionally in `on_step`, and `:722` runs `grug_mounts.warning_state(player, pos)` once a second. The design accepts the 112-sample sweep (`docs/design/mounts.md` around line 500), so this is a cost note, not a defect.
- **Proposal:** Cache the per-step answer per column, as the town check does (`record.flight_x/z`, rounded the way `normalize_coordinate` rounds: half away from zero). That helps hovering and slow descent. For the sweep, consider a small per-faction column cache shared by riders, or reuse the minimap's flight-tile cache (`grug_map base.lua flight_overlay`). The dismount moment and the warning text must stay identical.
- **Effort:** S (step cache) / M (sweep) · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before and after: 20 riders on Master flyers circling over home land at seed 4242424242, time spent in `on_step` and `warning_state` over 60 s.
- **Confidence:** medium

## Backlog notes

- Chest per player and member rotation: leaving the party frees a place (`runs.lua:333-341`), and every distinct character who enters after the kill gets a full chest roll (`chest.lua:85-100`; design expeditions.md §10 allows "also a member who came in after the kill"). A party can therefore cycle any number of characters (alts) through one boss kill. The design sanctions it, so it is noted here for the economy lane.
- Wipe revives the dead members (`packs.lua:336-356`, design §8). Leaving the party takes a player off a list without penalty (`runs.lua:576-585`, design §3), so a two-member party can farm a pack's first kills repeatedly with no death penalty. Rewards equal open-world mobs at the chosen level.
- A living player still standing in a stale box when a new run's preparation starts there is respawned home (`preparation.lua:98-106` → `runs.lua:657-660`) instead of being sent to their saved origin. This happens only within the second between login and the trip out.
- `respawn_lock.lua:174`: a `locks[name]` entry of a player who logs out dead and never returns lives until restart (tiny).
- `runs.lua:176-183`: `mark_deserter` writes the meta only for an online player. All callers are online today (cancel, accepting an invitation).
- `grug_expeditions:origin` keeps a waystone id across a faction change. A faction change while the origin is set would send the way back to the other faction's stone (`runs.lua:312-323`, `guard.lua:109-117`). Whether faction changes reach this state was not traced.
- Claim Stone form reach is 10 nodes (`stone_form.lua:43`), but travel and Expedition entry from it need `rules.near`, 8 nodes (`waypoints_core.lua:13, 44-48`). At 8-10 nodes the form opens but its Travel or Enter button refuses.
- `navigation.lua:237, 307` and `walkgrid.lua:290` use `^` (`2 ^ (k - 1)`, `(x - a.x) ^ 2`). The code is not deterministic, so this is allowed, but `bit.band` or `x * x` would be cheaper on the per-search path.
- `traps.lua:426`, `:395-401`: every 0.2 s tick allocates a new `players` and `state.last` table per busy run.
- `interface.lua:138-147`: the 10 s character-status poll walks all players in one step (cheap per player).
- Engine behaviour not checked (empty `reference_projects/luanti`): respawn_lock.lua's reliance on builtin `death_screen.lua` calling `core.show_death_screen` at a death, at a join with hp 0 and on a closed form; ObjectRef equality in `entity.lua:677` (`player:get_attach() ~= self.object`).

## Good to know

- The Expedition lifecycle holds on every path traced: create → enqueue → ready → enter (arrival sets `entered`) → teleport hook → inside; expel, end, logout, restart login, logout death, death in a box with the run alive or ended, slot reuse with dead or living players in the box, respawn while the run ends (`guard.lua` hands it on). Every in-memory table (`states`, `by_run`, `boxes`, traps and particles state, `views` weak) is dropped on `register_on_end`. Fireballs are `static_save = false`. Stale pack bodies on disk remove themselves at activation (`packs.lua:456-474`).
- Crafted fields are safe: an `enter_<id>` sent from the actions-only window of a stone inside an Expedition is refused by `grug_home.waystone_pos("expedition:<id>")` → nil ("That is no way in.", `runs.lua:613`). The `act_*` fields act only on a button the window offered (`waypoints.lua:412-425`).
- grug_home/travel.lua is solid: one pending token per trip; join, leave, death and a newer trip invalidate it; 30 s timeout; respawn always ends somewhere; the cooldown is charged only on an exact landing. The respawn hold is released on join.
- Housing map reset: the claims are cleared at startup through `grug_core.map_reset.clear` (`api.lua:25`, `registry.lua:211-225`). Players with a placed stone become `needs_stone`. grug_home's claim target is cleared on relocate and again at join by `deliver` (`claim_home.lua:216-244`). The recovered-belongings storage is player-bound and survives by design.
- The mount restore and grug_home's respawn use separate movement-hold ids (`grug_mounts:restore`, `grug_home:respawn`), so neither releases the other.
- grug_map's minimap `static_key` calls `grug_home.get`, `known_waypoints` (meta read + decode) and `claim_waypoint` per player on its SLOW (5 s, phased) tick. That falls under P4-03's cache if one is added.
