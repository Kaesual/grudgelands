# M3 — Mapgen wp40: R5/R6 settlements, planner, placement

- Finder model: claude-fable-5-2
- Files read in full: 12 of 13; skimmed: `kit_templates.lua` lines 330-455 (the structure builders `grave_mound` .. `driftwood`, pure data-like code; the rest of the file was read); not reached: none
- Summary: The package is deterministic as far as static reading shows: no `^`, no `math.random`, `os.clock` only for timings, every `pairs` either copies map to map or is sorted before output, and the placement design text matches the code. The one determinism defect is the surface-skin opening, which reads the VoxelManip shell and so depends on emerge order. The most valuable items are per-chunk costs that can go without changing a byte: the commit scan's double classification of every modified voxel for metrics nobody reads, the surface selector run two to three times per column by two separate selector objects, full-volume copy and reset passes, and the strata pass's per-depth hashes. About 900 lines of `r6_settlement.lua` (evidence, capture, replay) and the surface-cave writer are unreachable from `mods/` and from `tools/`. Several items were already raised by `docs/audit-2026-10/code/01-mapgen-terrain.md` (MGT ids are named where they are still open); MGT-02 (planner halo) and the traceback part of MGT-01 are fixed.

## Findings

### M3-01 | Medium | determinism | The surface-skin opening reads the VoxelManip shell, so a border column's result depends on emerge order

- **Where:** `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r6_settlement.lua:55-94` (`r9_native_air_run`, `r9_surface_skin_open`), `:2462-2499` (`skin_context`), `:2500-2533` (the per-column decision)
- **What:** Whether a land column's top is opened into a native cave (state 2) or sealed with a stone skin (state 1) is decided from `original_data` at positions outside the owner: the 3 x 3 neighbour columns (`x`/`z` in the 16-node shell for owner-edge columns) and depths 1-4 below a ground within four nodes of the owner's bottom. The shell holds a neighbour chunk's final content when that chunk was generated first and something else (ignore or untouched v7) when it was not, so the same seed gives a hole or a skin depending on which chunk the emerge thread made first.
- **Evidence:** `:2468-2474` `native_class_at` answers from `original_data[index_at(x, y, z)]` for any position inside the emerged bounds `eminx..emaxx`; `:86-92` rejects the opening when a neighbour's air run is under 4; `:2484-2491` treats `y < min_y` as "already the committed result of the preceding slice" (an order assumption); `:2511-2517` the pre-check reads `y >= eminy`. A generated neighbour in state 1 has filled its depths 1-3 with stone (`:2650-2674`), an ungenerated one has not. The module guide forbids exactly this: "Do not move cave connection decisions into the planner or infer them from emerge order" (`docs/technical/modules/mapgen.md:546-547`). Raised as MGT-09 in October, still present. Engine behaviour not checked (the `reference_projects/luanti` submodule is not checked out); the defect holds whether a fresh shell block reads ignore or native v7 content.
- **Proposal:** Decide the opening from order-free inputs only: the planned column tuple and native air read strictly inside the owner; treat a neighbour column or a depth outside the owner as "not open" (or as the planned value). Output changes for a few columns per world at chunk borders; everything else stays identical.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** medium · **Upgrade class (guess):** map reset
- **Confidence:** medium

### M3-02 | Medium | perf | The commit scan classifies both sides of every modified voxel, mostly for counters no production code reads

- **Where:** `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r6_settlement.lua:3516-3590`, `:1387-1390`; `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r7_content.lua:434-443`
- **What:** For every owner voxel whose content or param2 changed, the last pass calls `classify` twice (nine results each, two `integer()` range checks and a counter per call in `classify_runtime`) to derive `light_changed` and `liquid_changed`, and keeps doing so after both are already true, only to fill the per-column `light_columns`/`liquid_columns` counters and `metrics_state`.
- **Evidence:** `:3539-3544` the two `classify` calls inside `for z / for x / for y` over the 80^3 owner; a surface chunk changes tens to hundreds of thousands of voxels (all R5 fill and clear). The counters go to `metrics_state` (`:3582-3590`), read only by `settlement.metrics` (`:3666-3675`), which is reached only through `session.metrics` in `r6.lua:274-294`; `rg "session\.metrics|built\.session" mods` finds no call of it: the only uses of the built session are `r7_mapgen.lua:102-103` (`plan_slice`, `writer.apply`); the other hits define the zones and height sessions' own `metrics`. Runs once per generated chunk in the emerge thread.
- **Proposal:** In runtime mode memoise the pair decision by `old_cid * 65536 + new_cid` (light and liquid depend only on the two content ids; the liquid level matters only for liquids, which set the flag anyway) and skip classification once `light_changed` and `liquid_changed` are both true. `content_changed`, `param2_changed`, `light_changed`, `liquid_changed` and therefore every VoxelManip call stay identical; only the unread column counters change.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured; `tools/mgprof` on one fixed seed, a 5 x 5 ring of surface chunks (one flat, one river, one forest area): time of the commit loop before and after, identical chunk digests.
- **Confidence:** high

### M3-03 | Medium | perf | The surface row of a column is selected two to three times per chunk by two separate selector objects

- **Where:** `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r6_content.lua:771-1193` (`new_surface_selector`, steep memo `:909-959`); callers `r6_planner.lua:142`, `:341`; `r6_settlement.lua:1188`, `:197`, `:1717`, `:2579`, `:2622`
- **What:** The planner and the writer each build their own selector (`new_surface_selector` returns a new closure with its own row tables and its own 65,536-slot steep memo), so every column's selection (three to four value-noise evaluations of four lattice hashes each, `coast_material_at`, `start_ground_at`, the steep class with eight column lookups, in a third of the columns four more lookups, the snow class) runs once in `column_tuple`, again in the strata pass, and again for each `analytic_p7_*` call; each vertically stacked chunk repeats all of it.
- **Evidence:** `r6_planner.lua:341` (6,400 columns per chunk), `r6_settlement.lua:197` (every admitted land column), `:1717` (`analytic_p7_material_ref`: once per decoration candidate at `:2887`, per sealed top at `:2579`, per snowy column at `:2622`, and per P9G and world-content query through `successor_context.analytic_p7_ref`). `vegetation_rule` and `swamp_pools` in the same file are already shared per seed (`r6_content.lua:750-770`); the selector is not. The selection is a pure function of (x, z) and the world: every caller passes the column's own tuple values.
- **Proposal:** Share one selector per seed (as `vegetation_rule` is) and give it a direct-mapped per-column memo of the selected row, keyed like the steep memo and guarded by the stored `id`, `water_y` and `terrain_y`. In `r6_settlement.lua:2622` test `rbase`/opcode 26 before the analytic call. Rows and output stay byte-identical; one steep memo instead of two.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured; mgprof, same seed and ring as M3-02, `plan_slice` plus strata time and the count of selector calls per chunk before and after.
- **Confidence:** high

### M3-04 | Medium | perf | Each chunk pays about five full passes over the 112^3 emerged volume that carry no information

- **Where:** `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r6_settlement.lua:2369-2374`, `:2377-2403`
- **What:** After `get_data`/`get_param2_data` the writer copies both buffers into `final_*` and zeroes five side arrays over all 1,404,928 emerged voxels, although only the 512,000 owner voxels are ever written in the side arrays; then the nested R5 adapter is handed a shadow VM whose `get_data`, `get_param2_data`, `set_data` and `set_param2_data` each copy the whole volume again into and out of the adapter's own two buffers.
- **Evidence:** `:2369-2374` one loop, seven stores per voxel; `:2379-2392` four full-volume copy loops; `map_adapter.lua:603-604`, `:1225-1231`, `:1413-1416` (the adapter's `data_buffer`/`param2_buffer` exist only to receive and return those copies). - **Proposal:** (a) reset the five side arrays over the owner box only; (b) let the adapter read and write `final_data`/`final_param2` directly (the shadow's `get_*` return the retained buffers, `set_*` become no-ops), dropping two 112^3 arrays and four copies. - **Effort:** M · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured; mgprof on any chunk (the cost is independent of content): `apply` time up to the first P7 write before and after; identical chunk digests; emerge heap after a full collection.
- **Confidence:** high

### M3-05 | Medium | perf | The strata pass draws three column hashes per depth and does its column prelude for columns it cannot touch

The pass also runs `static_exclusion_values_at`, the protected floor and `select_surface` for every owner column before looking at `y`, also in chunks where the column's top 40 nodes lie wholly above or below the owner (every chunk under a mountain).

- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
