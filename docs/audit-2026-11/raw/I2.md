# I2 — Quality, gear, gathering, repair, professions, stations

- Finder model: claude-opus-5-5
- Files read in full: 39 of 41; skimmed: `mods/ITEMS/grug_gathering/catalog.lua` (data rows and the canonical-bytes framing, hashed mapgen catalog), `mods/ITEMS/grug_alchemy/recipes.lua` ingredient rows (read, not checked row by row against item_tiers.md §5); not reached: none
- Summary: The package is in good shape on correctness: grug_quality really is the one store path (every enchant, upgrade, crown, roll and authored stack goes through `store_affixes`), it works on ItemStack copies where it must, the repair transaction re-validates exact stacks through `grug_money.take_with_inventory`, and the sampled numbers (enchant curve, drop tiers, weapon curve, crown, trinkets, durability budgets, repair price) match item_tiers.md, items_crafting.md and durability_repair.md. The real cost sits in the description pipeline: a gear tooltip is assembled in three layers (grug_quality, grug_gear's weapon line, grug_repair's decorate) and stored per stack with player-specific text, so every Str/Dex or level change rewrites weapon stacks and, when the equipped weapon is among them, re-runs the whole equipment-change callback chain a second time (I2-01); repair's extra decorate pass even reorders the lines and causes one more rewrite (I2-02). Every gear stack also carries derived meta keys and a roll seed nobody reads (I2-03). The station mods in this package are thin node definitions; the station logic lives once in `grug_jobs/workspaces.lua` (node timers, contents dropped on dig/blast), but `grug_brewing` secretly registers all five grug_jobs profession stations through a dofile of `grug_jobs/station_nodes.lua` (I2-05), and three copies of the station node shape exist (I2-07). The rest is small dead code, stale docs and minor per-tick work (fishing bobber, Scout-shot wear, armor per hit).

## Findings

### I2-01 | Medium | perf | Weapon tooltip refresh re-runs the whole equipment-change chain and walks every inventory list twice

- **Where:** `mods/ITEMS/grug_gear/init.lua:806-830` (refresh_weapon_descriptions), `:837-855` (its two hooks), `:751-801` (initialize_weapon_tooltip); `mods/CORE/grug_core/combat.lua:362-395` (notify, nested pass)
- **What:** The "Effective at level N: D damage per swing" line is stored in each weapon stack's meta and depends on the viewer's level and melee bonus (Str or Dex / 10, `grug_classes/stats.lua:102-106`). The equipment-change consumer walks *every* list of the player inventory (main, four bags, hotbar store, quiver, belt, equipment ...) and rewrites each weapon whose line changed. When the equipped weapon or offhand is among them it calls `grug_inventory.equipment_changed` from inside the callback loop; grug_core then runs the *whole* consumer list a second time (stats apply, visuals compose and `set_properties`, Character page resend, ability-skin pass, trinket cache, repair hints, and this walk again). Equipping or removing any item with a Str/Dex enchant, an equipped Str/Dex item breaking, or a level-up therefore costs two full consumer passes plus two full inventory walks; a level-up costs a third walk (the level hook calls `equipment_changed` itself, which re-enters the consumer).
- **Evidence:** `init.lua:817-821` `if grug_gear.initialize_weapon_tooltip(stack, player) then inventory:set_stack(...) if listname == "grug_weapon" or listname == "grug_offhand" then equipment_changed = ...`; `:825-829` calls `inventory_api.equipment_changed(player, ...)` with no reason; `combat.lua:372-381` records a nested call as pending and `:385-394` re-runs `run_equipment_callbacks(player, nil)`. The description-only rewrite changes no stat, look or skin input (the image change case is separately reported by `appearance_changed`). Also `:812-816` copies every list with `get_lists()` and then copies every stack again with `get_stack`.
- **Proposal:** Only announce an equipment change from this consumer when the stack's *appearance* changed (`appearance_changed` in initialize_weapon_tooltip; return it separately from "description changed"); a description-only rewrite of an equipped weapon needs `set_stack` but no notification (or a narrow reason such as the existing `durability_metadata`, whose consumers already return early). Iterate the `stacks` table that `get_lists()` already returned instead of calling `get_stack` again. Must stay identical: the stored description text, the image refresh after a break, and the ability skins (check that `grug_abilities/init.lua:2392ff` does not mirror the weapon description; it states it reads "pool, description or skin" on full changes, so confirm before choosing "no notification" over the narrow reason).
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before/after on any seed: one player with 60 filled slots (10 weapons), equip/unequip a ring with a Str enchant 100 times; count `run_equipment_callbacks` invocations and total time in `grug_core.notify_equipment_change` (expected 2 passes -> 1).
- **Confidence:** high

### I2-02 | Medium | simplify | Gear tooltip assembled in three layers; repair's extra pass reorders the lines and forces one more rewrite

- **Where:** `mods/ITEMS/grug_quality/init.lua:514-550` (regenerate_description: adds "Usable by", requirement, durability, calls `refresh_appearance`), `mods/ITEMS/grug_gear/init.lua:751-801` (initialize_weapon_tooltip: `refresh_appearance` again, regenerate, then `repair.decorate_description`, then the Effective line), `mods/ITEMS/grug_repair/presentation.lua:68-92` (decorate_description and refresh_stack: `refresh_appearance` a third time, initialize, then decorate again)
- **What:** For one weapon `refresh_stack` runs `refresh_appearance` three times and builds "Usable by" (8 `hand_accepts` calls) three times. Worse, the final `decorate_description` in `refresh_stack` strips and re-appends "Usable by" and "Durability" *after* the Effective line was appended, giving `..., Requires, Effective, Usable by, Durability`, while `initialize_weapon_tooltip` produces `..., Usable by, Durability, Effective`. The next equipment-change refresh sees a different description and rewrites the stack; if equipped, this triggers the nested second consumer pass of I2-01. `refresh_stack` runs on every repair (`service.lua:312`), on a break (`runtime.lua:112` fallback when broken), on tool `on_use` and foreign `after_use` (`runtime.lua:242,268`). A repaired weapon left in a bag keeps the odd order until the next refresh.
- **Evidence:** `presentation.lua:83-90` (`initialize_weapon_tooltip` then `decorate_description` on the result); `gear/init.lua:771-775` (decorate inside initialize) and `:793-795` (Effective appended last); `quality/init.lua:535-543` (regenerate already appends usable_by and the durability line, and calls `repair.refresh_appearance`). Every repair-eligible item has a quality family (weapons, shields, spellbooks, armor, pick/axe/shovel/hoe all return a family in `family_for`, `quality/init.lua:260-287`), so for them the decorate passes only re-do regenerate's work.
- **Proposal:** Make `regenerate_description` the one assembler of the shared lines (it already is for family items) and drop the two `decorate_description` calls for stacks with a family (keep it only for the on_mods_loaded definition text, `runtime.lua:224`); let `refresh_stack` be `refresh_appearance` + (`initialize_weapon_tooltip` or `regenerate_description`). Must stay identical: the tooltip text for every item path as produced by `initialize_weapon_tooltip` today.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** high (line order), medium (that no non-family eligible item exists outside grug_gear/grug_materials/grug_farming)

### I2-03 | Low | persist | Every gear stack stores derived stat keys and a roll seed that nothing reads

- **Where:** `mods/ITEMS/grug_quality/init.lua:19-22,160-177,328-339` (`_grug_strength` ... `_grug_attack_speed_percent`), `:659` (`grug_roll_seed`)
- **What:** `write_derived` clears nine keys and writes one per enchanted stat on every `store_affixes` and every `regenerate_description` (so on every tooltip refresh of every gear stack). No mod reads these keys from a stack: equipment totals come from `read_affixes` (`:1155`), capabilities from the returned totals. `grug_roll_seed` is written on every roll and never read. With the per-player description (~0.4-0.5 KB), the enchant image string (~0.2 KB), serialized `grug_ench` (~0.15 KB) and tool capabilities (~0.15 KB) a rolled gear stack carries roughly 1 KB of meta (estimate, unmeasured); the dead keys are a small but pure part of it.
- **Evidence:** `rg "_grug_strength|_grug_dexterity|_grug_intelligence|_grug_max_hp_percent|_grug_crit_percent|_grug_dodge_percent|_grug_armor_rating|_grug_attack_speed_percent|_grug_max_mana_percent" mods` outside grug_quality finds only the *definition* field `_grug_max_mana_percent` of spellbooks (`grug_gear/init.lua:401,615`, read from `def`, not meta); `rg grug_roll_seed mods tools --type lua` finds only the writer. Only `tools/r50_paccept/evidence` and `tools/r45_ms/evidence` JSON snapshots contain the names.
- **Proposal:** Stop writing the derived keys and the seed (keep `write_derived`'s totals computation as a pure function); optionally clear them once in `regenerate_description` so stacks shrink as they are refreshed. Update the header comment `:7-22`. Must stay identical: capabilities and totals.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** high

## Good to know

- grug_quality's one store path holds: `store_affixes` (`quality/init.lua:613-624`) is reached from rolls, enchant plans, upgrade plans, the crown and `make_stack`; nothing else writes `grug_ench` or `grug_ilvl` (`rg "grug_ench|grug_ilvl" mods` outside grug_quality reads only). Plans work on `ItemStack(stack)` copies (`:713,775,840,942,979`); `roll_enchants` and `crafted_output` mutate the stack they are given by contract.
- Sampled numbers that match the specs: enchant curve and coefficients (`quality:122-143` vs item_tiers.md §1.1), dropped-enchant tier (`ceil(ilvl/10)`, T7 above 60, equals `min(7, floor((ilvl-1)/10)+1)`), crown rule and cap (`:811-846` vs §4), upgrade cap and refusals (`:741-780` vs §3.1), weapon curve with two-step rounding (`gear:266-280` vs items_crafting.md §3.2, e.g. Battle Axe at 42 = 29), trinket values (`trinkets.lua` vs items_crafting.md table at line 1587ff), combat durability 1000-4000 (`repair/presentation.lua:8`), repair price `ceil(0.50 x P x w)` with quality 1/3/6 (`service.lua:229-236`, `gear:483-505`), death wear 10 % and the PvP exemption (`repair/init.lua:10-13`, `runtime.lua:191-213`).
