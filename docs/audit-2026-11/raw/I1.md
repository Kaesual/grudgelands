# I1 — Decor, materials, nodes, trees, signs, farming

- Finder model: claude-opus-5-5
- Files read in full: 45 of 46; skimmed: `mods/ITEMS/grug_nodes/props_data.lua` (generated data table, header and first rows read; `props.lua` consumes it); not reached: none
- Summary: The package is in good shape. Node timers drive the crops, soil, salt pan and saplings. The two farming LBMs are idempotent current-version activations. There is no ABM. The tool aliases are exactly the twelve allowed ones, plus one legal Round 48 item alias. The sampled tool, tier and farming numbers match `items_crafting.md`, `durability_repair.md` and `farming.md`. Three earlier fixes (ITM-02 tall-crop orphans, ITM-03 right-click order, ITM-10 slab-on-slab) are in place. The most valuable remaining items are: (1) a crop whose planter lost the right to the cell never grows again, and it flashes a protection line to that player every 15 s from anywhere in the world; (2) the tool lifetime and capability values are written in four places and then overwritten by `tool_lifetimes.lua`, so tuning the visible tables does nothing; (3) dead data and registrations (edits of unregistered blocks, engine fuel rows nobody reads, a roster with no consumer). The renewal sapling guard still scans its 21³ box against a list of about 800+ names, and that cost grows with every new node.

## Findings

### I1-01 | Medium | edge | A crop that lost its planter's right never grows, and every retry flashes "protected" to the planter wherever they are

- **Where:** `mods/ITEMS/grug_farming/init.lua:239-243` (`protection_ok` records a violation), `:245-254` (`transition_crop` checks the root and every helper cell against the planter), `:311-315` (a refused growth retries after `SOIL_INTERVAL`), `:454` (a harvest makes the harvester the "planter"), `:579-591` (the geometry LBM calls the same path); `mods/ITEMS/grug_materials/mining.lua:622-629` (every recorded violation by an online player becomes a flash)
- **What:** Timer-driven growth checks protection under the stored planter name. Every refusal calls `core.record_protection_violation(pos, planter)`. This happens even on single-node crops, because the root cell itself is checked at `:250`. Suppose the planter no longer may change that cell. Examples: a player with "everything" rights planted in someone's claim and lost the right, or a claim became active over a field. Then the crop stays at its stage forever. Its timer re-fires every 15 s. Each firing sends the planter, if online anywhere, the claim's protection line (e.g. "Home of X – protected") for a position they are not near.
- **Evidence:** `crop_timer` → `transition_crop(pos, state, next_stage, player_name)` (`:311-312`) → `protection_ok(pos, player_name)` (`:250`) → `core.record_protection_violation` (`:241`) → `register_on_protection_violation` handler → `grug_materials.emit_hint(name, protection_hint(pos, name))` (`mining.lua:622-629`). Claim protection refuses non-owners inside an active claim (`mods/PLAYER/grug_housing/protection.lua:1-8`, `:42-48`). On refusal, `crop_timer` re-arms at `seconds - SOIL_INTERVAL` (`:313-314`), so the next attempt comes 15 s later. This is still present after `docs/audit-2026-10/code/09-items.md:482-483`, where it was noted without action.
- **Proposal:** Growth is not a player action. Give the timer and LBM paths a "silent" protection check that refuses without recording, for example a flag on `transition_crop` that skips `record_protection_violation`. Keep the recording for `harvest_crop` and `dig_crop`. Also decide whether the root cell (the crop's own cell, already occupied) needs a check at all on the growth path. Only the helper cells change new space. What stays identical: player harvest and dig refusals, and the no-partial-mutation rule.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** high

### I1-02 | Medium | dup | Tool lifetimes and capability values are written in four places and overwritten by `tool_lifetimes.lua`; tuning the visible tables changes nothing

- **Where:** `mods/ITEMS/grug_materials/mining.lua:34-53` (`PICK_PROFILES[t].uses`, `punch_attack_uses`), `:141-144` (refuses `punch_attack_uses <= 0`), `:166-167` (`damage_groups {fleshy = 4}`); `mods/ITEMS/grug_materials/tools.lua:77-93` (`AXE_PROFILES`/`SHOVEL_PROFILES` `uses = 24…60`), `:127`, `:148` (axe/shovel damage); `mods/ITEMS/grug_materials/tool_lifetimes.lua:3-21` (the real values); `mods/ITEMS/grug_farming/hoes.lua:5`, `:11-20` (a third copy of `{300 … 3000}` and its own wear-remainder arithmetic)
- **What:** `tool_lifetimes.lua` overwrites every groupcap `uses` of every pick, axe and shovel. It sets `punch_attack_uses = 0` and `damage_groups = {fleshy = 0}`, and the real lifetime comes from its own `lifetimes` list (`_grug_tool_uses`, read by `grug_repair`). So the profile `uses`, `punch_attack_uses` and damage values are dead on arrival. `build_pick_capabilities` still validates `punch_attack_uses > 0`, a value that is always thrown away. `hoes.lua` repeats the tier lifetime list and the wear-remainder arithmetic that `grug_repair/runtime.lua` also holds.
- **Evidence:** `tool_lifetimes.lua:10-13` (`caps.damage_groups = {fleshy = 0}`, `caps.punch_attack_uses = 0`, `cap.uses = …`), `:15-21`. No reader of `PICK_PROFILES[..].uses` exists outside `build_pick_capabilities` and `audit.lua:299-327`: `rg -n "PICK_PROFILES" mods` gives `wp43_handoff.lua:142` (type check only), `audit.lua`, `tools.lua` comment. `grug_repair/presentation.lua:5-6` and `runtime.lua:239` read `_grug_tool_uses`/`_grug_hoe_uses`. This is ITM-05 of `docs/audit-2026-10/code/09-items.md:102`, still open.
- **Proposal:** Keep one per-tier lifetime table in `grug_materials`, and let `hoes.lua` read it, plus `{30, 60}` for wood and stone. Remove `uses`/`punch_attack_uses`/`damage_groups` from `PICK_PROFILES`, `AXE_PROFILES`, `SHOVEL_PROFILES` and from `build_pick_capabilities`' validation, or have `build_pick_capabilities` take the final lifetime so `tool_lifetimes.lua` only normalizes groups and the description. What must stay identical: the final `tool_capabilities` of every tool (dig times, maxlevel, the final `uses`), `_grug_tool_uses`, and descriptions. The engine dig matrix in `audit.lua` proves the times are unchanged. The WP43 projection pins `PICK_PROFILES` only by type (`wp43_handoff.lua:142`), not by digest. Check this before removing fields.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** high

### I1-04 | Low | perf | The renewal sapling guard scans a 21³ box against the whole non-natural name list, a cost that grows with every registered node

- **Where:** `mods/ITEMS/grug_farming/renewal.lua:284-293`; the list is built at `mods/ITEMS/grug_farming/init.lua:658-665`
- **What:** Before a woody plant is placed, `find_nodes_in_area(21³, api.non_natural)` runs. The engine resolves every name per call (`collectNodeIds`) and then does a linear `std::find` over the id vector for every one of the 9,261 visited nodes (`reference_projects/luanti/src/script/lua_api/l_env.cpp:820-880`, `findNodesInArea`). Most visited nodes are natural (air, stone, dirt), so they miss and scan the whole vector. Round 49 logs show 788 names (`tools/r49_jit/evidence/boot1/server.log:102`). Every decor round adds more. That is about 7-10 M id comparisons in one call, inside one globalstep slice.
- **Evidence:** Hot path: the globalstep slice every 0.5 s (`init.lua:671-678`) services each player once per 5 s. A guard runs only after a passed woody roll (`renewal.lua:277-293`), so it is rare but bursty. The R10 renewal bursts are already in BACKLOG; the previous audit noted this at `docs/audit-2026-10/code/09-items.md:474-480`.
- **Proposal:** Replace the guard query with one `VoxelManip` read of the 21³ box and a Lua boolean lookup indexed by content id, built once in `on_mods_loaded` from the same `natural_node` rule. Or test the natural complement with an id set. Behaviour (which spots are refused) must stay identical.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. On a larger machine, time `renewal.evaluate(spot, positions, {left = 150000}, 0, "tree")` with a forced roll on seed 42 at a forest spot 30 nodes from the probe player, before and after (µs per call), with the node count printed by the boot log line `init.lua:666-668`.
- **Confidence:** medium

## Backlog notes

- `dig_crop` returns nil on every path, refused ones included (`grug_farming/init.lua:413-437`). The engine reads nil as "dug" (`reference_projects/luanti/src/script/cpp_api/s_node.cpp:136-137`). The server ignores the value (`serverpackethandler.cpp:1127`), so this matters only for a mod's `core.dig_node`.
- `dig_crop` bypasses `core.node_dig`, so no `register_on_dignode` runs for crops (`grug_jobs/basic_unlock.lua:395-397` `held_dirty`). The 10 s sweep covers it.
- `place_seed` runs `start_crop_timer` twice: once through `on_construct` and once explicitly (`grug_farming/init.lua:362-364`). `hoe_on_use` likewise starts the soil timer twice (`hoes.lua:40-42`; noted before).
- `soil_on_timer` reads the node above twice: `soil_timer`, then `soil_interval` (`grug_farming/init.lua:117-138`). One `get_node` per soil check.
- Compat branch for crop timers started under 0.69.0 (`grug_farming/init.lua:305-310`). It is legal (post-0.41), but it can go once no 0.69 world remains.
- Mapgen-placed town field soil (`grug_mapgen/wp13/palette.lua:283`) runs the 60 s water check forever (`grug_farming/init.lua:134-157`) on protected fields where nobody can plant. The cost is CPU only: node timers do not mark blocks modified (`reference_projects/luanti/src/mapblock.h:393-396`).
- Natural digs ask `core.is_protected` twice, in `mining_decision` and in builtin `node_dig` (`grug_materials/mining.lua:399-407`, `:654`).
- Exported symbols with no game consumer: `grug_farming.SOIL_INTERVAL`, `WATER_RADIUS`, `STAGES`, `salt_pan`, `RENEWAL` (`grug_farming/init.lua:36-38`, `:552`, `:630`; `STAGE_SECONDS`/`stage_seconds`/`renewal` are used only under `tools/`); `grug_materials.ANTHRACITE_TORCH` (`torch.lua:100`); `grug_trees.shape_nodes`/`shape_woods` (`shapes.lua:91-92`, only under `tools/`).
- Furnishings carry no `attached_node`, so digging the table or wall under a plate or sconce leaves it floating (`grug_decor/furnishings.lua:148-172`). Paintings do attach (`paintings.lua:53`). Possibly intended.
- A 2×2 painting is one node. Its other three cells stay free after the placement check, so they can later be built into, or overlapped by a second painting (`grug_decor/paintings.lua:11-37`).
- The notice board and the Anthracite torch call `udef.on_rightclick` directly instead of `grug_core.node_rightclick` like the other custom placers (`grug_signs/init.lua:318-321`; `grug_materials/torch.lua:66-75`).
- `hanging.after_dig` swaps the piece above into an end without a protection check (`grug_trees/hanging.lua:143-147`). The change is cosmetic.
- `grug_trees/mod.conf` depends only on `default`, but the code calls `grug_core` at runtime (`init.lua:64`, `:286`) and `dofile`s grug_mapgen's `wp40/tree_templates.lua` + `treegen.lua` (`init.lua:47-56`). That is a fourth in-memory instance after `r7_runtime.lua:178-179`, `wp13/dressing.lua:310-311` and `wp13/city_green.lua:106`. It cannot be declared because of the dependency cycle.
- Capital props: `on_dig = nothing` returns nil (= dug) (`grug_decor/capital.lua:20`). This is harmless because `diggable = false`.
- Shared `groups`/`sounds` tables across many registrations (`grug_decor/furnishings.lua:59-64`, `:166-168`; `castle.lua:82-91` shallow `copy_merge`). Safe while nobody mutates a registered `groups` in place. Current in-place writers all work on copies (`rg "def\.groups\.[a-z_]+ *=" mods`).
- `max_drop_level` is 1 on the Bronze and Steel picks (`overrides.lua:330-350`) and 0 on the Iron, Silversteel, Embersteel and Abyssal picks (`tools.lua:108-112`). No drop table depends on it today.
- `place_variant` rebuilds the stack from its name and count (`grug_nodes/plants.lua:97-103`). Fine for metadata-free decor items only.

## Good to know

- ABMs: none in the package. LBMs: `grug_farming:start_soil_timer` and `grug_farming:activate_crop_geometry` (both `run_at_every_load`, idempotent, no writes when nothing is missing) and `grug_signs:drop_hover_text` (see I1-05). Node timers: crop roots, soil, the salt pan, and saplings through vendored `default.grow_sapling`. Growth catches up offline through the engine's elapsed time, stage by stage (`grown_stage`), as `farming.md` states. Paused crops have no timer, and the soil check under them always resumes them, so no crop timer can be lost.
- Aliases: exactly the twelve `grug_materials.TOOL_ALIASES` (4 grades × pick/axe/shovel, `tools.lua:27-48`, audited in `audit.lua:264-269`). One more, `grug_farming:seed_salt_crust → grug_cooking:salt_crust` (`grug_farming/init.lua:550`), is a Round 48 rename after 0.41.0 and so allowed by AGENTS.md "Stable ids".
- Numbers checked against the docs by sample, all matching: tier y bands and `TOOL_LEVEL_REQUIREMENTS` 5/15/25/35/45; gem density 512/1024/2048 and crystal 512/256; hoe and tool lifetimes 30/60/300…3000; crop stage 160–240 s, soil 15 s / 60 s, water radius 3, salt pan 4 × 200 s; renewal rates (0.05 / ×4 shore, 0.5, 0.1, 150 000 visits, 5 s).
- Verified fixed since audit-2026-10: ITM-02 (`after_destruct`/`on_flood` remove helpers, `grug_farming/init.lua:518-525`), ITM-03 (`grug_core.node_rightclick` first in seeds, bucket, salt pan, waterlily, hanging plants, furnishings, paintings), ITM-10 (slabs consumed only on `placed`).
- Sign writes are safe. `default.node_formspec` checks range and node identity server-side (`mods/BASE/default/node_formspec.lua:133-166`). The text is escaped in the form, limited to 512 bytes and stripped of control characters (`grug_signs/init.lua:100-117`). Sign text costs one non-saved entity per surface (two per waypost) with a `[combine` texture whose length grows with the text. That cost belongs to `grug_text` (package outside I1).
- `grug_trees`' sapling growth and `grug_farming/renewal.lua` are pure modules with injected APIs; their fixtures live under `tools/`.
