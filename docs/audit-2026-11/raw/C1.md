# C1 — grug_core: combat, movement, zones, protection, world version, migrations

- Finder model: claude-fable-5-2
- Files read in full: 36 of 37; skimmed: `mods/CORE/grug_core/particle_effects.lua` (the emitter catalogue; helpers and registration structure read, the 100 emitter lists skimmed); not reached: none
- Summary: The package is carefully written and heavily commented; the world version guard, the map reset and the migration runner match `docs/technical/upgrade-contract.md` §3 and §5.3-5.6 at every point checked, and no crash or data-loss defect was found. The valuable items are per-player and per-hit costs that grow with the 100-player target: the 0.5 s status pass (every player in one step, the list built three times), `status_modifier_sum` allocating and sorting inside every crit and armour read, the nametag carrier pass (carriers x players, with player-meta reads eight times a second), and `get_properties()` in the aim eye, the homing step and the environment tick. One rule gap: `grug_core.world_alterable` does not know the road/village/POI layer, so water and growth may enter cores that AGENTS.md says are never damaged. `reference_projects/luanti` is not checked out here, so every engine statement below is "engine behaviour not checked" and rests on the code's own citations.

### C1-01 | Medium | security | `world_alterable` ignores the road, village and POI layer, so water and growth may enter protected cores

- **Where:** `mods/CORE/grug_core/protection.lua:39-44` and `:82-85`; `mods/CORE/grug_core/water_guard.lua:25-33`, `:82-92`, `:108-118`; compare `mods/CORE/grug_core/protection.lua:50-53` and `mods/CORE/grug_core/zone_authority.lua:533-541`
- **What:** `world_alterable(pos)` is the territory rule (`accord_home`, `throng_home`, `contested_land`) plus the registered guards. It never asks `grug_core.world_feature_at(pos)`, the layer that protects roads, bridges, villages, camps, fortresses, war camps, mining camps and POI cores for every player in `core.is_protected`. Those cores lie in home or contested territory, so for the water guard, tree growth and plant growth they count as alterable. A player can pour a bucket one node outside a village or POI core (not protected there for the own faction) and the water flows into the core, washing away floodable nodes; AGENTS.md "Terrain-damage guard" says towns, starts, capitals and POIs are never damaged and names `world_alterable` as the question to ask.
- **Evidence:** `protection.lua:82-85` (`world_alterable` = `natural_ground_alterable` and not guarded); `protection.lua:39-44` (territory only); `zone_authority.lua:540` (`world_protected_for_faction` adds `world_feature_at(pos) ~= nil` on top of the faction policy, i.e. the policy alone allows these positions); `protection.lua:50-53` (`ground_growth_allowed` had to add `world_feature_at(pos) == nil` itself, Round 37); `water_guard.lua:26` (`if grug_core.world_alterable(pos) then return false end`: flow is not guarded there). Callers of `world_alterable` in `mods/`: `water_guard.lua:26`, `mods/BASE/default/functions.lua:244,270`, `mods/ITEMS/grug_farming/bucket.lua:20`, `mods/ITEMS/grug_trees/init.lua:64` (rg over `mods/`). `zone_authority.lua:518-521` shows the layer was left out on purpose for one system effect (the dragon's ground effect), so the intent for water and growth needs a ruling.
- **Proposal:** Decide the rule, then make code and AGENTS.md agree. If cores are meant to be safe: let `world_alterable` also refuse where `world_feature_at(pos)` is set (the planned-flow predicate in `water_guard.lua:29-31` already carries the world's own water), and keep `ground_effect_protected` as the dragon's separate path. Must stay identical: water inside claims, planned canal and river flow, the barrier revert, behaviour in towns and capitals.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** medium · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** medium (the code path is certain; whether roads and cores were meant to be open to water is the open point)

### C1-02 | Medium | perf | The status pass handles every player in one step and builds the status list three times per player

- **Where:** `mods/CORE/grug_core/status.lua:358-373` (`advance_statuses`), `:394-418` (`refresh_hud`), `:464-475` (globalstep), `:217-242` (`each_status`), `:284-325`
- **What:** Every 0.5 s one globalstep walks all connected players. Per player `advance_statuses` calls `each_status` twice (each allocates `ordered` and `expired` and sorts), then `refresh_hud` -> `status_display` -> `status_effects` calls it a third time, asks every status source, allocates `entries`, `taken`, `visible` and one table per shown effect, and sorts two or three more times. A player with no status and no source entry pays all of it. This is the pattern AGENTS.md "Performance" rules out ("no pass handles every player or zone in one step").
- **Evidence:** `status.lua:471-474` loops `core.get_connected_players()` without slots; `:359` and `:372` call `each_status`; `:286` calls it again; sorts at `:235`, `:323`, `:333`, `:338`; `:401-402` calls `core.get_player_window_information(name)` for every player with at least one icon, every pass. At 100 players: 200 player passes per second.
- **Proposal:** (1) One `each_status` per player per pass: tick from the list already built and re-sweep only when a tick ran. (2) Skip the sort and the row build when the player has no stored status and no source reports one. (3) Spread players over slots as `atmosphere_zones.lua:604-644` does, keeping 0.5 s per player. (4) Read the window information only when the shown count changed. Must stay identical: the tick catch-up arithmetic (`:365-369`), expiry after the final tick (`should_expire`), row order, and the send-on-change HUD writes.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** medium · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before/after with 100 stand-in players, half with two timed statuses (one ticking): time of the status globalstep per pass and Lua allocation (`collectgarbage("count")` delta) over 60 s.
- **Confidence:** high

### C1-03 | Medium | perf | `status_modifier_sum` allocates and sorts on every crit, armour and spell-damage read (the per-hit path)

Read the level once in `scale_player_damage` and pass it on. Must stay identical: which statuses count at a given time, the `on_expire` and modifier-change notifications on a sweep.

### C1-09 | Medium | bug | A Lua error inside an ability punch is caught and logged as a warning; the hit pipeline stops half way without a severe report

- **Where:** `mods/CORE/grug_core/combat.lua:1306-1319`
- **What:** `deal_ability_damage` wraps `target:punch` in `pcall` to restore its flags, then logs `[grug_core] ability punch failed` at warning level and returns 0. - **Proposal:** Keep the flag restore, then either raise the error again (`error(err, 0)`, the rule every other hook here follows) or report it through `grug_core.severe.report("grug_core", "ability punch failed", err, key)` with a key per message. Which of the two is the user's choice.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** high
