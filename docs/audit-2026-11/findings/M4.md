### M4-01 | Medium | perf | Every chunk transaction builds a race palette, closures and a ledger for all 144 settlements

- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED — traced `r7_mapgen.lua:113-114` (`refine`, after the two `air_chunks` early-outs) -> `writer.apply` -> `r6_settlement.lua:3353` (`successor_tail:settle`, once per transaction) -> `r7_successor.lua:127-129` (all roster rows) -> `r7_settlement.lua:1581`. `approach_palettes.new(profile.race)` at 1620 is unconditional and uncached (only `prepare_approaches` at 1526-1527 is memoized), and `wp13/palette.lua:1017-1021` runs `contains` over the required and optional lists for every bound role (quadratic in ~115 roles), i.e. on the order of 10^4 string compares per settlement and 10^6 per transaction, before the collar/active tests. `r7_settlement.lua` and `wp13/palette.lua` are not in the preparation digest, so the guess "compatible" stands.
- **Where:** `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r7_settlement.lua:1581-1766` (`tail.settle`, notably 1597-1633 and 1763-1765), called for every roster row by `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r7_successor.lua:127-129`; the palette constructor `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp13/palette.lua:981-1028`
- **What:** `settle` does its setup before it knows whether the settlement touches the chunk: a footprint string from four numbers (1597), `approach_palettes.new(profile.race)` (1620), five role lookups (1621-1624), two closures (1603, 1625) and a result table (1763). `palette.new` walks about 115 roles and, for each role of the race, scans the required and optional lists linearly (`contains`, palette.lua:954, 1018-1021). The roster has 144 rows (6 starts, 6 capitals, 18 R14, 70 R20, 18 PvP, 26 camps), and at most one or two are near any chunk.
- **Evidence:** `r7_successor.lua:127` `for index = 1, #settlements do ledger[settlements[index].key] = settlements[index]:settle(context) end`; `r7_settlement.lua:1620` `local fit_palette = approach_palettes.new(profile.race)` is unconditional; the only consumers of `fit_palette`, `fit_write` and `reach` sit under `collar_work` (1634) and `state.active` (1676, 1703). Called once per chunk transaction that passes `air_chunks` (`r7_mapgen.lua:100-103`).
- **Proposal:** in `settle`, compute "any state active" and the reserved-square overlap first and return the constant ledger when neither holds; resolve the five approach refs once in `config.new` (they depend only on `profile.race` and `refs`). Keep the write order (collars, cells in blueprint order, edge) and the `write_span` rule as they are. Output byte-identical. (Verifier: the early return must still bump `metrics.settle_calls`/`replay_calls` as 1757-1762 do, and `reach` is also fed by the overlay branch, so the `write_span` record at 1754-1756 only happens when a branch ran; returning early when no branch can run keeps it identical.)
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible (no digested file touched)
- **Measure:** unmeasured; emerge time per chunk transaction far from any settlement, before/after, same seed and area (the Round 49 probe area), plus one capital chunk column to confirm identical node hashes.
- **Confidence:** high

### M4-02 | Medium | perf | P9G scan reads every column once per catalogue row, twice when it is land

- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED — `r7_p9g.lua:557-567` calls `context.column_values_at` per row, cell and column on a column-cache miss (12 rows, `rows = catalog.p9g_sources()` at 89), and `geographic_reason` (313-314) reads it again for every column with `surface_y >= 1`; `column_values_at` is the 20-value cached lookup at `zones.lua:1576-1592` (normalize, two table indexes, 20 returns). The scan runs on the first vertically active chunk of each chunk column (`plan_slice` 423-437 sets `bound_vertical_active`; cache at 247-262/521-531), so once per chunk column, not per chunk. The 80x80 owner gives exactly the 25 cells x 256 columns cited. Byte identity holds: the four values are pure per column. `r7_p9g.lua` is not digested.
- **Where:** `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r7_p9g.lua:557-585` (scan), `:313-333` (`geographic_reason`)
- **What:** On a column-cache miss the scan runs 12 catalogue rows x 25 cells x 256 columns and calls `context.column_values_at(x, z)` per row and column for `surface_y` (566), then again inside `geographic_reason` (314) for water class, zone and biome. All four values are row-independent.
- **Evidence:** loop `for catalog_index = 1, cached and 0 or #rows` (557) around the cell and column loops (559-567); `context.column_values_at` forwards to `planner_source.column_values_at` (`r6_settlement.lua:3234-3236`), a cached query that normalizes x/z and returns 20 values (`zones.lua:1576-1592`). That is 76,800 calls plus up to 76,800 more per chunk column; the Round 49 column cache (247-262) only spares the column's other vertical chunks.
- **Proposal:** read the 6,400 columns once into scratch arrays (surface y, water class, zone id, biome) before the row loop; let `geographic_reason` take them as arguments in the scan and keep its own read for `settlement_decision` and `probe_reason`. Keep the row, cell and column loop order, the rank draws, budgets and the sort. Output byte-identical. (Verifier: the surface y is already in `plan.column_values[(i-1)*12+5]`, which `plan_slice` reads at 428; only water class, zone and biome need the extra pass.)
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible (no digested file touched)
- **Measure:** unmeasured; settle time of the first surface chunk of a column (`column_cache_misses` unchanged), before/after on one seed over a fixed 5x5 chunk-column area.
- **Confidence:** medium

### M4-03 | Medium | perf | Main rebuilds and hashes every blueprint on each boot although the layout cache hit

- **Verdict:** CORRECTED — the cost is real: on a hit `W.capital_plots = {}` (`world_assembly.lua:220-221`) and `r7_runtime.lua:276-277` passes `prepared_handover or capital_plots`, `prepared_handover` being nil in main (`r7_loader.lua:94-99` passes nil as the 9th argument), so `r7_settlement.prepare` (952-968) builds and hashes every blueprint of all 144 settlements; the handover is plain data (`r7_settlement.lua:1013-1034`, `plain_copy`) whose inputs are the seed and the mapgen tree (`blueprint_options` at `r7_runtime.lua:216-217`), both in the cache key (`r7_loader.lua:57-78`). The upgrade class is wrong as proposed: relaxing the guard at `r7_runtime.lua:656` is a byte change to a digested file, which stops every full-preparation world at its next boot. Corrected proposal keeps the change in `r7_loader.lua` and `layout_cache.lua` (neither digested).
- **Where:** `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r7_runtime.lua:258-298` (the `prepare` loop, 276-277), `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/world_assembly.lua:220-221`, `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r7_loader.lua:94-119` and `:133`
- **What:** The world-folder cache stores the four layout texts only (placement, water, road, capital; `r7_loader.lua:460-465`). On a hit `W.capital_plots` is `{}`, so `r7_settlement.prepare` finds no cached preparation and builds every blueprint of all 144 settlements again (six capital cores of about 100,000 cells, every district plot, every POI), writes one identity line per cell and hashes it, then drops the cells of the lazy ones. On a miss main is cheaper here, because the plots were already prepared for the planner. Emerge skips exactly this work by taking main's `prepared_blueprints` handover.
- **Evidence:** `world_assembly.lua:220` `W.capital_plots = texts.capital == nil and variant.protection == nil and A.capital_plots() or {}`; `r7_runtime.lua:276` `r7_settlement_module.prepare(profile, source, raw_sha256, prepared_handover or capital_plots)`; `r7_settlement.lua:952-968` builds whenever the cache has no entry; `r7_loader.lua:133` builds the handover and `:387` ships it to emerge; the hit line at `:452-458` logs this remaining time as "runtime construction".
- **Proposal:** store `prepared_blueprints` as a fifth section of the layout cache (same key, same "any failure rebuilds" rule; it needs a structured serializer, the other sections are text) and, on a hit, pass it to the `r7_runtime.lua` constructor as its existing `prepared_handover` argument (`r7_loader.lua:96-98`), keeping the cached copy in the loader for the payload and `r69_camp_sites` instead of calling `runtime.prepared_handover()` (whose guard at `r7_runtime.lua:656` then stays untouched). The other uses of a non-nil `prepared_handover` in `r7_runtime.lua` hold in main too: the seam check at 36-42 needs only a capital text (present on a hit), and 302-320 checks that the handover holds exactly the roster's lazy blueprints. Emerge's check of every lazy rebuild against the identity (`r7_settlement.lua:1443-1455`) stays the safety net, but in main nothing rebuilds lazy cells, so a stale cached handover would only be caught in emerge; the cache key covering the whole mapgen tree is what keeps it fresh. Side effect: main still builds the six starts (eager, not in the handover). Output byte-identical.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** medium · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible if confined to `r7_loader.lua`/`layout_cache.lua`; as the finder proposed (editing `r7_runtime.lua`) it stops worlds in full preparation mode (preparation authority digest)
- **Measure:** unmeasured; the "runtime construction %.1f s" figure of the cache-hit log line before/after on the same world, and the manifest sha256 unchanged.
- **Confidence:** medium
