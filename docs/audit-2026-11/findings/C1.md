- Summary: The package is carefully written and heavily commented; the world version guard, the map reset and the migration runner match `docs/technical/upgrade-contract.md` §3 and §5.3-5.6 at every point checked, and no crash or data-loss defect was found. ### C1-01 | Medium | security | `world_alterable` ignores the road, village and POI layer, so water can flow into protected roads and cores

- **Verdict:** CORRECTED — the code gap holds (`protection.lua:39-44`, `:82-85`; the layer is only in `zone_authority.lua:540` and `protection.lua:50-53`), but the reach is narrower than stated (water is the real path; trees and the bucket are covered by player protection) and the proposal misses the water barrier, without which the fix reintroduces the Round 37 air-revert loop at every core edge.
- **Where:** `mods/CORE/grug_core/protection.lua:39-44` and `:82-85`; `mods/CORE/grug_core/water_guard.lua:25-33`, `:70-75`, `:82-92`, `:108-118`; compare `mods/CORE/grug_core/protection.lua:50-53` and `mods/CORE/grug_core/zone_authority.lua:533-541`
- **What:** `world_alterable(pos)` is the territory rule (`accord_home`, `throng_home`, `contested_land`) plus the registered guards. Towns, capitals and start towns (`zones.lua:1182-1186` "hard_protected"), R7 landmark columns (`r7_zone_overlay.lua:50-53`) and Miner's Guild rooms (`guild_room_protection.lua:28-31`) answer "hard_protected" in `territory_rule_at` and are safe. Roads (centreline half width + 1 node, +-5 around the surface), bridges, and the village, camp, fortress, war camp, mining camp and POI core boxes (10 below placement to 10 above the top, `wp40/world_protection.lua:1-40`) live only in `world_feature_at`; the territory rule there is home or contested, so `world_alterable` answers true. The only world alteration guard registered in `mods/` is the Claim Stone's arrival cube (`grug_housing/protection.lua:96-98`). What each caller can write into that layer today:
  - **Water flow** (`water_guard.lua:25-33`, both `on_flood` and `on_liquid_transformed`): reachable. A player pours a bucket one node outside a core or beside a road where `core.is_protected` allows it (own home or contested land); the flow enters the core box or the road band, fills air and floods floodable nodes there (torches, plants, decor) and is never reverted. This contradicts AGENTS.md "Terrain-damage guard" ("POIs are never damaged") and the header of `protection.lua:5-6` ("authored/immutable content is not mutable for water or plants").
  - **Cactus and papyrus growth** (`mods/BASE/default/functions.lua:244`, `:270`): reachable only where a cactus or papyrus already stands in the column inside or just below a core box (up to 4 nodes of growth); the comment at `functions.lua:205-208` ("never grow in a town or POI") is wrong for POI cores.
  - **Sapling growth** (`mods/ITEMS/grug_trees/init.lua:64`): mostly covered, because placing a sapling checks `core.is_protected` (which includes the layer) over the whole growth volume (`grug_trees/init.lua:82-90`, `default/trees.lua:487-496`, `is_area_protected` sampled at interval 4); a thin road band between sample points was not checked.
  - **Bucket** (`mods/ITEMS/grug_farming/bucket.lua:20`): not a gap; it asks `core.is_protected` first, which already refuses the layer for every player.
  - Natural renewal uses `natural_renewal_allowed`, not `world_alterable`; its exclusions of settlements, POIs and roads come from the habitat writers (`grug_farming/renewal.lua:212-228`) and are out of this finding.
  The intent is genuinely open: `wp40/world_protection.lua:1-3` states the layer is "for player digging and placing only" (Round 25 rulings 15-17), while Round 37 (`ground_growth_allowed`) and the comments above extend it to growth; `zone_authority.lua:518-521` leaves it out on purpose for the dragon's ground effect. The rule needs the user's ruling.
- **Evidence:** as above; callers of `world_alterable` in `mods/` (rg): `water_guard.lua:26`, `default/functions.lua:244,270`, `grug_farming/bucket.lua:20`, `grug_trees/init.lua:64` — five, as the finder said. Registered alteration guards: `grug_housing/protection.lua:96` only.
- **Proposal:** Get the user's ruling first, then make code and AGENTS.md agree. If cores and roads are meant to be safe from water: let `world_alterable` (or only the water guard's `guarded`) also refuse where `world_feature_at(pos)` is set, keep the planned-flow predicate winning there (`water_guard.lua:29-31`) and keep `ground_effect_protected` as the dragon's separate path. **Also** extend `water_guard_revert_node` (`water_guard.lua:70-75`) so air reverted on a world-feature position becomes the barrier, as on hard-protected ground: the layer is fixed for the world's life like the territory rule, and a plain air revert there loops once a second with a block resend (the Round 37 CORE-02 measurement described at `water_guard.lua:35-50`). Must stay identical: water inside claims, planned canal and river flow, the barrier revert on hard-protected ground, behaviour in towns and capitals, the bucket's own refusals.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** medium · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible (barriers left in the world are `grug_air`, read as air by the world writer)
- **Confidence:** high for the water path; the intent is the open point

### C1-02 | Medium | perf | The status pass handles every player in one step and builds the status list three times per player

- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED — `status.lua:464-475` walks `core.get_connected_players()` every 0.5 s with no slots; `advance_statuses` calls `each_status` at `:359` and `:372`, `status_display` -> `status_effects` a third time at `:286`, plus four status sources (`movement.lua:595`, `grug_pvp/hud.lua:14`, `grug_traders/potion.lua:76`, `grug_expeditions/registrar.lua:405`); `get_player_window_information` builds a five-field table per call (`l_server.cpp:274-313`). For a player with no status each call is small (empty tables, empty sorts), so the cost is mainly allocation churn and one step spike at 100 players.
- **Where:** `mods/CORE/grug_core/status.lua:358-373` (`advance_statuses`), `:394-418` (`refresh_hud`), `:464-475` (globalstep), `:217-242` (`each_status`), `:284-325`
- **What:** Every 0.5 s one globalstep walks all connected players. Per player `advance_statuses` calls `each_status` twice (each allocates `ordered` and, when the player has statuses, `expired`, and sorts), then `refresh_hud` -> `status_display` -> `status_effects` calls it a third time, asks every status source, allocates `entries`, `taken`, `visible` and one table per shown effect, and sorts two or three more times. A player with no status and no source entry still pays the allocations. This is the pattern AGENTS.md "Performance" rules out ("no pass handles every player or zone in one step", AGENTS.md:312).
- **Evidence:** `status.lua:471-474` loops `core.get_connected_players()` without slots; `:359` and `:372` call `each_status`; `:286` calls it again; sorts at `:235`, `:323`, `:333`, `:338`; `:401-402` calls `core.get_player_window_information(name)` for every player with at least one icon, every pass. At 100 players: 200 player passes per second.
- **Proposal:** (1) One `each_status` per player per pass: tick from the list already built and re-sweep only when a tick ran. (2) Skip the sort and the row build when the player has no stored status and no source reports one. (3) Spread players over slots as `atmosphere_zones.lua:604-644` does, keeping 0.5 s per player. (4) Read the window information only when the shown count changed. Must stay identical: the tick catch-up arithmetic (`:365-369`), expiry after the final tick (`should_expire`), row order, and the send-on-change HUD writes.
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** medium · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before/after with 100 stand-in players, half with two timed statuses (one ticking): time of the status globalstep per pass and Lua allocation (`collectgarbage("count")` delta) over 60 s.
- **Confidence:** high

### C1-03 | Medium | perf | `status_modifier_sum` allocates and sorts on every crit, armour and spell-damage read (the per-hit path)

- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED — `status.lua:88-98` builds the sorted list via `each_status` for a sum; callers verified at `grug_classes/stats.lua:62,68,134,147,155`, `grug_inventory/equipment.lua:876`, `grug_quality/init.lua:1256`, crit rolls at `combat.lua:1063,1271,1370`. `scale_player_damage` reads the level twice (`combat.lua:164`, `:168-169`) and `deal_ability_damage` again (`:1290-1291`); each read is a `get_meta()` userdata allocation plus `get_int` plus the linear `level_from_xp` loop (`grug_xp/init.lua:104-110`, `:130-136`). Per call the cost is microseconds; it matters through area skills hitting many targets.
- **Where:** `mods/CORE/grug_core/status.lua:89-99`, `:217-242`; callers `mods/PLAYER/grug_classes/stats.lua:143-148` (crit), `:133-135` (spell damage), `mods/PLAYER/grug_inventory/equipment.lua:873-877` and `mods/ITEMS/grug_quality/init.lua:1256` (armour); combat sites `mods/CORE/grug_core/combat.lua:1063`, `:1270-1271`, `:1370`, `:1627`
- **What:** `status_modifier_sum` calls `each_status`, which allocates a list (and an `expired` list when the player has statuses) and runs `table.sort` with a comparator, only to add one field over the records. It runs inside `get_crit_chance` (every ability hit, melee swing and heal), inside the armour rating (every punch on a player) and inside the spell damage percent. Order does not matter for a sum. In the same path `scale_player_damage` reads `grug_core.get_player_level(player)` twice and `deal_ability_damage` a third time; each read is a player-meta read plus the level curve (`mods/PLAYER/grug_xp/init.lua:104-110`, `:130-136`).
- **Evidence:** `status.lua:94` (`local ordered = grug_core.each_status(player)`), `:235` (`table.sort(ordered, status_order)`); `combat.lua:164` and `:168-169` (two level reads), `:1290-1291` (third); crit and dodge accessors are uncached (`stats.lua:160-169`, `:204-205`).
- **Proposal:** Sum directly over `statuses[name]` with the same expiry test, without building or sorting a list (remove an expired record exactly as now). Read the level once in `scale_player_damage` and pass it on. Must stay identical: which statuses count at a given time, the `on_expire` and modifier-change notifications on a sweep.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before/after: 10 000 calls of `grug_core.deal_ability_damage` against a stand-in mob by a player carrying three statuses, time and allocation; and a 20-player area-skill burst probe.
- **Confidence:** high
