### I2-01 | Medium | perf | Weapon tooltip refresh re-runs the whole equipment-change chain and walks every inventory list twice

- **Verdict:** CORRECTED — the equip/break path holds as written (two full consumer passes, two inventory walks); the level-up count is wrong (one consumer pass, two walks, not three), and the open point is settled: no mod mirrors the weapon description, but the swing clock compares the whole stack with `equals`, so "no notification" would cost a swing; use the narrow `durability_metadata` reason. The current double pass also restarts the swing clock on every level-up and Str/Dex equip (a side effect the finder missed).
- **Where:** `mods/ITEMS/grug_gear/init.lua:751-801` (initialize_weapon_tooltip), `:806-830` (refresh_weapon_descriptions), `:837-855` (its two hooks); `mods/CORE/grug_core/combat.lua:370-400` (notify, nested pass); `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/init.lua:1743-1758,2392-2441` (swing clock)
- **What:** The "Effective at level N: D damage per swing" line is stored in each weapon stack's meta and depends on the viewer's level and melee bonus (`grug_classes/stats.lua:102-106`). The gear consumer (registered in `on_mods_loaded`, so after every load-time consumer) walks every list of the player inventory and rewrites each weapon whose line changed; when the equipped weapon or offhand is among them it calls `grug_inventory.equipment_changed` with no reason.
  - **Equip/unequip of a Str/Dex item, or a break of one** (verified chain): `equipment_changed` → `notify_equipment_change` pass 1 (stats, talents page, visuals, abilities, trinkets, Character page, repair hints, gear walk 1) → the equipped weapon's line changed → nested `equipment_changed` recorded as pending (`combat.lua:372-382`) → pass 2 with `listname = nil` over all consumers (`:385-394`), including gear walk 2 (no change, stops). Two full passes, two walks.
  - **Level-up** (corrected): `grug_xp` level callbacks → gear's level hook (`init.lua:850-854`) walks once → the equipped weapon's line changed → `equipment_changed(player, "grug_weapon")` → one full consumer pass (not nested, nothing else on the level path notifies: `rg equipment_changed mods/PLAYER/grug_classes mods/PLAYER/grug_xp` finds no caller) → gear walk 2. One pass, two walks; there is no third walk.
  - **Side effect found while verifying:** in that extra pass, grug_abilities' consumer compares its swing snapshot with the live weapon by `ItemStack:equals` (`grug_abilities/init.lua:2429`), which includes meta; the description rewrite makes it unequal, so the swing clock restarts at a full interval (`next_due = now + fpi`). A level-up mid-fight or equipping a Str/Dex ring therefore delays the next melee swing by up to one weapon interval.
  - Also `:812-816` copies every list with `get_lists()` and then copies every stack again with `get_stack`.
- **Open point settled:** no consumer copies or depends on the weapon's description text. `grug_abilities.update_stack_description` (`grug_abilities/init.lua:1225-1236`) builds ability text from player stats and the weapon's capabilities, not its description; `sync_skins` reads item names; visuals compose from player state; the Character and talent pages render lists whose tooltips come from the stack itself; the remaining `get_description` readers (`rg 'get_string\("description"\)|get_description\(\)' mods`) are on-demand formspecs and feed lines, not equipment consumers. But a description-only rewrite with **no** notification would leave the abilities swing snapshot stale and the next swing (`grug_abilities/init.lua:1750-1758`) would take the rewritten weapon for a swap and return false (a lost swing). The `durability_metadata` branch (`:2393-2407`) refreshes the snapshot for the same item name, which is exactly what is needed.
- **Proposal:** In `refresh_weapon_descriptions`, notify with `reason = "durability_metadata"` (and the hand's list name) when an equipped hand's stack changed only in description; keep the full notification when `initialize_weapon_tooltip` reports an appearance change (return the two separately). All other consumers already return on that reason or rebuild cheaply (trinkets rebuild only for `nil`/trinket lists; repair hints compare state per slot). Iterate the `stacks` table that `get_lists()` returned instead of calling `get_stack` again. Must stay identical: stored description text, the image refresh after a break (full notification), ability skins. Behaviour change (wanted): a level-up or Str/Dex equip no longer restarts the swing clock.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. One player with 60 filled slots (10 weapons), equip/unequip a ring with a Str enchant 100 times; count `run_equipment_callbacks` invocations (expected 2 → 1 pass per equip) and check the swing clock's `next_due` across a level-up.
- **Confidence:** high

### I2-02 | Medium | simplify | Gear tooltip assembled in three layers; repair's extra pass reorders the lines and forces one more rewrite

- **Verdict:** CORRECTED — the line reorder and the triple `refresh_appearance`/`usable_by` work hold as written (`presentation.lua:80-91`, `gear/init.lua:762-775,793-799`, `quality/init.lua:535-543`); one citation was wrong (`runtime.lua:112` is a wear call; the break fallback is `presentation.lua:112`) and the proposal as written would change visible text: `regenerate_description` emits "Usable by, Requires, Durability", while today's decorated output is "Requires, Usable by, Durability".
- **Where:** `mods/ITEMS/grug_quality/init.lua:514-550` (regenerate_description), `mods/ITEMS/grug_gear/init.lua:751-801` (initialize_weapon_tooltip), `mods/ITEMS/grug_repair/presentation.lua:68-91` (decorate_description, refresh_stack), `:112` (refresh_durability's break fallback)
- **What:** For one weapon `refresh_stack` runs `refresh_appearance` three times (refresh_stack, initialize, regenerate) and builds "Usable by" three times (regenerate and two decorates). The final `decorate_description` in `refresh_stack` strips and re-appends "Usable by" and "Durability" after the Effective line, giving `..., Requires, Effective, Usable by, Durability`, while `initialize_weapon_tooltip` produces `..., Requires, Usable by, Durability, Effective`. The next equipment-change refresh rebuilds from scratch, sees a different text and rewrites the stack; if equipped, that adds the nested second consumer pass of I2-01. Between rewrites, per-use `refresh_durability` only swaps the Durability line, so the odd order persists in bags.
- **Proposal:** Make `regenerate_description` the one assembler of the shared lines and drop the two `decorate_description` calls for stacks with a quality family (keep it for the on_mods_loaded definition text, `runtime.lua:211`); `refresh_stack` becomes `refresh_appearance` + (`initialize_weapon_tooltip` or `regenerate_description`). To keep the tooltip identical, `regenerate_description` must put the requirement line before "Usable by" (today's visible order after decorate is Requires, Usable by, Durability, then Effective for weapons). Eligible items without a family (any `grug_equip_head/chest/legs/feet` item lacking `grug_armor_class`, `repair/service.lua:6-15` vs `quality/init.lua:260-287`) keep the decorate path.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** high (line order), medium (that no non-family eligible item exists)
