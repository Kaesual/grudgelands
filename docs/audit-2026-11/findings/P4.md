### P4-01 | Medium | edge | Flyer set-down at login teleports the player back to the logout column after a teleport or respawn

- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED — traced the restore path step by step (below). `/home` lands during the set-down and the next `look` moves the player back to the old column, after the home trip has already charged its 30-minute cooldown (`travel.lua:214-217`); a death followed by a respawn before the next `look` does the same. Engine checked: a respawn needs the `__builtin:death` form's button (`reference_projects/luanti/builtin/game/death_screen.lua:40-42`, `player:respawn()`), so the death case needs a quick click while `look` is waiting on its emerge or 1 s retry; the `/home` case needs only an off-cooldown player out of combat. Rare path, real player loss (position and cooldown): Medium fits.
- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_mounts/restore.lua:98-151` (also `:153-175`, `:177-193`)
- **Trace (verifier):**
  1. Join (`restore.lua:177-193`): a saved tier clears the key, sets `pending[name] = token`, holds movement (`grug_mounts:restore`: no movement, no gravity) and polls `attempt` every 0.25 s.
  2. `attempt` (153-175) re-reads the position each poll (so a teleport during these first 5 s is harmless: the summon is tried where the player now is), releases on death or on a quickbar mount, and after `RESTORE_WAIT = 5` s without an active block, or on a refused summon, sends a flyer to `set_down`.
  3. `set_down` (98-151) captures `pos`, `x`, `z`, `top` once (100-104), emerges the column area (111-117) and runs `look` after the emerge, then every `SET_DOWN_RETRY = 1` s (143) for up to `SET_DOWN_TIMEOUT = 30` s; a separate 30 s `core.after` (147-150) only releases.
  4. `look` (119-144) checks only `pending[name] ~= token` and `hp <= 0`, then `set_pos` to the captured column's ground (125) or a neighbour (132). "harmful" with no safe neighbour returns without a retry (137), so that branch never moves the player back.
  5. Meanwhile `/home` (`grug_home/home_command.lua:17-30`) checks creation stasis, home, `is_pending` (grug_home's own trips only) and hp; `return_home` (`travel.lua:191-203`) adds combat, return guards and cooldown. Nothing looks at the mount restore or its movement hold. The trip emerges, `land` teleports home (`travel.lua:207-219`) and starts the cooldown. The next `look` (≤1 s plus emerge) finds ground in the old column and `set_pos`es the player there.
  6. Death: `grug_core/movement.lua:666` clears every hold on death (the restore hold too), `entity.lua:1014-1016` dismounts, but nothing touches `pending[name]` (`rg register_on_dieplayer mods/PLAYER/grug_mounts` finds only `trainer.lua:160` and `entity.lua:1014`). A `look` while dead releases (122); one after a quick respawn finds hp > 0 and moves the respawned player from `grug_home.respawn`'s held wait position back to the old column. If grug_home's `land` comes later it teleports them home again (`travel.lua:210-211`, since they are no longer at the arrival); if it came first, the player stays at the logout column.
  7. While all this runs, a player who reached home is still under the `grug_mounts:restore` hold until `look` or the 30 s last resort releases it.
- **What:** `set_down` stores `pos` once and later calls `p:set_pos({x = pos.x, y = ground.y, z = pos.z})`, or `p:set_pos(at)` for a neighbour column, from an emerge callback or a 1 s retry, up to 30 s later. It never checks whether the player has moved meanwhile. `/home` works during the hold, and so does a death followed by a quick respawn. Either one moves the player through `grug_home/travel.lua`, and the next `look` then teleports them back to the ground under the old logout position. The token is not dropped on death: `pending` is cleared only by `release`, join and leave, and `look` releases only when it runs while `hp <= 0`.
- **Evidence:** `restore.lua:100` `local pos = player:get_pos()`. `:119-126`: `look` checks only `pending[name] ~= token` and `hp <= 0` before `p:set_pos({x = pos.x, y = ground.y, z = pos.z})`. `:129-133` does the same for the neighbour columns. The file has no `register_on_dieplayer`. A refused restore is a common case: `attempt` sends every flyer whose block is not active within `RESTORE_WAIT = 5` s to `set_down` (`:164-173`), and that happens easily high over ungenerated air. Meanwhile the player hangs held in mid-air and will naturally type `/home`.
- **Proposal:** In `look`, release without moving when the player's horizontal distance to `pos` is more than about 1 node, or when the player is attached (the hold zeroes movement, so only a teleport moves them that far). Add `core.register_on_dieplayer` that drops `pending[name]` (the hold itself is already cleared by `grug_core.clear_movement`). A set-down that runs undisturbed behaves exactly as before.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Confidence:** high

### P4-02 | Medium | perf | Claim Stone check walks, copies and sorts every claim every 5 s in one step

- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED — `stone.lua:230-260` runs `draft_scan` (`registry.lua:699-709`), `expiry_scan` (`:745-756`) and `all_claims` (`:284-289`) in full every 5 s, each a `pairs` over every claim plus a sort, then one `get_node_or_nil` per claim and a second in `sync_stone_node` (`api.lua:226-238`); this breaks AGENTS.md:312 ("No pass handles every player or zone in one step"). Correction to the "What": the claim count is bounded by players ever, not by 30 days of fuel: a claim is one per player and is removed only by pick-up, destruction, admin removal or draft expiry (`rg "model.remove\(|M.remove\("`: `api.lua:352`, `stone.lua:162,216,253`, `admin.lua:23`); an expired claim stays, only marked by `expired_for`.
- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_housing/stone.lua:230-260`; `mods/PLAYER/grug_housing/registry.lua:284-289` (all_claims), `:699-709` (draft_scan), `:745-756` (expiry_scan)
- **What:** One globalstep runs three full passes over all claims every 5 s. Claims stay for offline owners after their fuel ran out, so on a long-lived server their number grows with every player who ever placed a stone, not with players online. The three passes are `draft_scan` (pairs over every claim plus a sort), `expiry_scan` (pairs over every claim plus a sort) and `all_claims` (a new array and a sort). Each claim then costs one `core.get_node_or_nil`, and a stone node costs a second one in `sync_stone_node` (`api.lua:226-238`). This breaks AGENTS "Performance": "No pass handles every player or zone in one step".
- **Evidence:** `stone.lua:236` `model.draft_scan()`, `:238` `model.expiry_scan()`, `:243-259` `model.all_claims()` plus `core.get_node_or_nil(claim.center)` and `grug_housing.sync_stone_node(claim)` (which reads the node again). Drafts are rare (5 min each) and expiries happen once per `paid_until`, yet both scans run in full every time.
- **Proposal:** Keep a draft set in the registry (add in `create`, drop in `activate` and `remove`) so `draft_scan` touches only drafts. Keep the next expiry time (minimum `paid_until` over claims not yet reported, updated by `add_fuel` and load) so `expiry_scan` returns at once until then. Spread the node-sync pass over slots (for example a fixed slice of the id-ordered list per 5 s step), or pass the node already read into `sync_stone_node`. Draft expiry and expiry events, their order by id, and the vanished-stone rule must stay identical (a sliced pass delays the vanished-stone detection of a claim by up to one full cycle; say so in the change).
- **Effort:** M · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before and after: a test world with 1,000 synthetic claims (registry stand-ins in mod storage) and one player, timing the globalstep (os.clock around the body) over 60 s.
- **Confidence:** high

### P4-03 | Medium | perf | Waystone discovery reads and decodes every player's meta once a second, all players in one step

- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED — `waypoints.lua:519-531` walks every connected player each second, `known_set` (64-66) reads and decodes the meta string, and `rules.newly_found` builds `M.network(rows, faction)` and a result list per call (`waypoints_core.lua:32-59`). The only writer of `grug_home:waypoints` is `waypoints.lua:73` (`rg` over `mods/`), so a per-name cache updated there stays exact; a later code path that writes the key (a character reset, a migration) would have to drop the cache entry. Severity: the per-player cost is small, but it is an AGENTS.md:312 breach with a cheap fix, so Medium stands.
- **Where:** `mods/PLAYER/grug_home/waypoints.lua:514-531` (with `:64-66` known_set and `waypoints_core.lua:32-59`)
- **What:** Once a second, for every connected player, the pass reads `player:get_meta():get_string("grug_home:waypoints")` and decodes it into a new set (a gmatch over the ids). It then builds a new network list per player (`rules.network`) and allocates a result list, all to test the stones against the player's position. The stored set changes only in `discover`, so almost all of this repeats. It is also one step for all players (AGENTS "Performance").
- **Evidence:** `waypoints.lua:521-529`: `for _, player in ipairs(core.get_connected_players())` → `known_set(player)` → `rules.decode(player:get_meta():get_string(KEY))`; `rules.newly_found` → `M.network(rows, faction)` allocates per call (`waypoints_core.lua:32-38`). For 100 players that is 100 meta reads and roughly 300 table allocations a second.
- **Proposal:** Cache the decoded set per player name: fill it at join (or first use), update it in `discover`, drop it at leave. Precompute the two faction networks once at load. Optionally skip a player whose own network's stones are all known: cache an "all known" flag. Spread the players over slots if the pass stays per player. Discovery timing and the stored text stay identical.
- **Effort:** S · **Risk:** low · **Upgrade class (guess):** compatible
- **Measure:** unmeasured. Before and after: 100 fake players (the r29_w probe's stand-ins), the globalstep's time over 60 s.
- **Confidence:** high

- A living player still standing in a stale box when a new run's preparation starts there is respawned home (`preparation.lua:98-106` → `runs.lua:657-660`) instead of being sent to their saved origin. This happens only within the second between login and the trip out.

## Good to know

- The Expedition lifecycle holds on every path traced: create → enqueue → ready → enter (arrival sets `entered`) → teleport hook → inside; expel, end, logout, restart login, logout death, death in a box with the run alive or ended, slot reuse with dead or living players in the box, respawn while the run ends (`guard.lua` hands it on). Every in-memory table (`states`, `by_run`, `boxes`, traps and particles state, `views` weak) is dropped on `register_on_end`. Fireballs are `static_save = false`. Stale pack bodies on disk remove themselves at activation (`packs.lua:456-474`).
- Crafted fields are safe: an `enter_<id>` sent from the actions-only window of a stone inside an Expedition is refused by `grug_home.waystone_pos("expedition:<id>")` → nil ("That is no way in.", `runs.lua:613`). The `act_*` fields act only on a button the window offered (`waypoints.lua:412-425`).
- grug_home/travel.lua is solid: one pending token per trip; join, leave, death and a newer trip invalidate it; 30 s timeout; respawn always ends somewhere; the cooldown is charged only on an exact landing. The respawn hold is released on join.
- Housing map reset: the claims are cleared at startup through `grug_core.map_reset.clear` (`api.lua:25`, `registry.lua:211-225`). Players with a placed stone become `needs_stone`. grug_home's claim target is cleared on relocate and again at join by `deliver` (`claim_home.lua:216-244`). The recovered-belongings storage is player-bound and survives by design.
- The mount restore and grug_home's respawn use separate movement-hold ids (`grug_mounts:restore`, `grug_home:respawn`), so neither releases the other; a death clears both (`grug_core/movement.lua:666`, `clear_movement`).
- grug_map's minimap `static_key` calls `grug_home.get`, `known_waypoints` (meta read + decode) and `claim_waypoint` per player on its SLOW (5 s, phased) tick. That falls under P4-03's cache if one is added.
