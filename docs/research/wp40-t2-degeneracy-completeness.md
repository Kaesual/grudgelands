Headline results:

1. **The locality hypothesis is false as stated but true in the form that
   matters.** Ten decisions are not functions of a bounded 8-neighbourhood
   (§1), but every one of them is a well-founded finite reduction with an
   explicit termination bound, and the single cross-family recursion
   (R19 → Bank tracing) is cut by a source-checkable invariant verified below
   against all 20 literal Bank records. Consequently every family's
   **decision space** is finite even though trace/face **configuration
   spaces** are not.
2. **The paper-undecided set is almost empty**: two text ambiguities in R19
   (U1, U2) and one vacuity proof obligation in the perimeter precedence (O1).
   Enumeration alone therefore does not predict the next R-correction —
   because the R-series was mostly not generated by undecided configurations
   (§2).
3. **The real generator is reject-class occupancy on wanted seeds**, and the
   wanted-seed universe is finite and known (§4). That converts the open-ended
   schedule into a bounded census program (§5) roughly two orders of magnitude
   cheaper than the 4,096-seed all-class scan.
4. **An acyclic freeze staging exists** up to one irreducible feedback edge
   that follows from two design axioms (scalar-only winner selection,
   no-fallback-seed), and the historically most expensive cycle — every
   correction invalidating the measured pool — is removable by scoping the
   pool pin to the scalar authority it actually consumes (§6).

## 1. Question 1 — the decision inventory and locality

Decisions that ARE bounded-local (8-neighbourhood plus declared identities):
station normals and local clip (§1.1), junction departure derivation (§2.1),
R16 transition resolution (E, diagonal W, two elbows; §3.2), R18 aperture
direct/tail resolution (D, A, W, two shoulders; §3.1), notch-fill
qualification (§7.1), attachment `A` selection (candidates constrained to
Chebyshev ≤ 1 of `E`, ties by station index; §4), bank-width station choice
(§7.2), relief profile mapping (§7.3), relief junction `J` (incident bands
only; §7.4). Section numbers are wp40-source-authority.md throughout.

Decisions that are NOT bounded-local, with their well-foundedness:

| Decision | Scope | Why it is well-founded |
|---|---|---|
| Record-wide topology ceiling `C` (§1.2) | whole record | Finite descending scan of at most `max_displacement+1` candidates; Stage 1 proves `C=0` valid, so termination with a result is unconditional; failure at `C=0` is invalid source, not a seed outcome. |
| Fixed mainland closure match (§1.3) | whole record | Finite authored segment set; exactly-one complete match required; every deviation is an enumerated reject (H55). |
| Post-ceiling envelope/width/cross-record connectivity (§1.3) | cross-record | Total verification predicates; reject, no retry. |
| R18 interval selection (§4) | whole edge vs. final water | Finite maximal-interval list; decision is a reduction to per-interval booleans and the count class {0, 1, ≥2}. |
| R19 joint tuple selection (§4) | interval × bay envelope | Candidates ≤ interval length − 1 per endpoint; checked-product tuple bound; ≤ 4 R11 traces per tuple with the per-bay `8N`/`N`/`N−1` caps; acyclic by the far-terminal invariant (below). |
| Bank trace step + reachability (§3.3) | trace history + bay envelope | Directed-state space ≤ 8·N; explicit frame/stack/step caps; every exhaustion is an enumerated reject; reversal never re-traces. |
| Wing K / tail pair / wedge (§3.4) | wing boxes | Finite boxes; path bound `ceil(isqrt(L))+1`; hard `R ≤ 5` (explicit reject in `bay_bank_reject`); total lexicographic selection. |
| Aperture interval extraction (design §7) | whole perimeter | Finite station sequence; wrap/overlap/second-run/boundary-predicate violations all reject. |
| Coast-source / nearest-feature (§5, design §13) | global roster | Pure minimization with a total tie order (zone ID → component ID → segment index). |
| Face/Whole validation (§2.2, §6.2) | whole footprint | Total predicates over a finite raster; every failure class rejects. |

**The one cross-family recursion and its cut.** R19 tuple completion runs R11
traces whose far terminals must already be authorized. The catalog encodes the
cut as policy (`bay_edge_transition_terminal_dependency`): each transition's
two incident Banks are exactly one start and one end, and each far terminal is
aperture- or Wing-derived, never an edge transition. Verified against the
literal 20 records — the five banks of each bay form the chain
`aperture(before) → transition → wing⁻ → wing⁺ → transition → aperture(after)`
(orientation differs per bay, the shape does not), so all 16 far terminals of
the 8 transitions are in {aperture_dry, wing_tail}, and each transition occurs
exactly once as a start terminal and once as an end terminal. Aperture
terminals derive from perimeter + final mask, wing tails are selected before
any trace, so terminal resolution is stratified: apertures/wings → transitions
→ banks → faces. No fixpoint iteration exists anywhere in the pipeline.

**Refined hypothesis (the load-bearing form).** Every policy decision is a
finite reduction of its (possibly global) input: either a bounded-local
predicate, a total tie order, a count-class reduction, or a bounded search
with enumerated exhaustion rejects. Hence the space of per-site *decision
classes* is finite and enumerable on paper — done in §3 — while the space of
realized *configurations* (trace shapes, face shapes) is finite per seed but
not enumerable seed-independently. The consequence for T2 is §4.

## 2. Question 2/3 preface — what actually generated the R-series

Classifying the thirteen corrections by mechanism:

- **(a) Reject-to-recovery promotions.** The configuration was explicitly or
  implicitly rejected; a wanted seed realized it; rejecting a wanted seed is
  unacceptable (R16's own rationale: "rejecting this seed would make the
  already-selected extreme corpus non-compilable"); the policy had to grow a
  recovery clause. R16 (non-candidate `E` → elbow), R18 (non-candidate `D` →
  shoulder tail; multi-interval edge → interval selection), R19 (dead direct
  terminal → candidate retreat with completion).
- **(b) Silent-wrongness-to-gate promotions.** The configuration passed but
  was wrong; a new invariant was added. R10 (raster topology ceiling), R11
  (masks are not shore polygons), R12 (trace anchors), R13 (junction
  overlap), R14 (junction categories), R15 (dry wedges), R17 (notches).
- **(c) False frozen universals.** A quantifier over all seeds was frozen and
  was false. R16 ("all-seed offset-zero claim"), H55 (all mainland stations
  movable coast).

Totality analysis (question 3) finds only the residue of (c) and any residual
(a)-cases that are textually undecided rather than explicitly rejected. It
cannot find future (a)-promotions — those are *explicitly decided* rejects
whose occupancy on wanted seeds is unknown — and it cannot find (b) at all.
Therefore §3 marks configurations DECIDED / REJECTED / UNDECIDED as asked,
and additionally flags REJECTED classes with plausible wanted-seed occupancy,
because the R-series says those are the actual future corrections.

recorded in [wp40-t2-plan.md](wp40-t2-plan.md) section 5).**
`bay_edge_transition_terminal_complete` *requires* a
nonempty combined contiguous control subsequence per tuple, while the R18-level
`shared_boundary_incidence_reject` lists an empty subsequence as
reject-without-fallback. The two readings diverge observably: on a seed where
one tuple has an empty combined clip and another tuple completes, the first
reading accepts (tuple merely fails), the second rejects the seed. Which is
authoritative is not stated.

**U2 (undecided at analysis time; decided 2026-08-16 — fails at the
tuple-level previous binding, recorded in
[wp40-t2-plan.md](wp40-t2-plan.md) section 5).**

or cross yields a degenerate combined clip and, in the limit, a single-station

ambiguity R16 was made of.

completion is selective enough to leave exactly one tuple; nothing on paper
bounds the number of complete tuples. This is the single most valuable census
quantity in §5.

### F3. Bank search states — decision-total, occupancy open

Sites: 20 components; 16 are completion-traced inside F2 tuples (the
transition-incident banks), 4 head banks (`whitebridge`, `lorindor`,
`speargrass`, `whispering` — both terminals wing tails) are first traced at
materialization.

Step decision: from `(previous, current)`, test 8 successors in fixed Moore
order against (candidate, unseen, ≠ previous, cardinal-water cross-sign,
terminal-reachable); select the first pass; zero passes reject. Every named
failure — dead end, repeated state/column, X-cross, foreign-water contact,
undeclared endpoint, frame/stack/step/envelope exhaustion — is an enumerated
reject in `bay_bank_reject`. The transition function is total: **no undecided
step configuration exists.** The step-class space (8 directions × predicate
bits) is finite by construction; what is not enumerable on paper is which
classes and which whole-trace shapes a seed realizes.

Residual risk, and the sharpest structural finding of this analysis — the
**terminal-kind completion asymmetry**:

| Terminal kind | Alternatives at resolution | Completion tested at selection? |
|---|---|---|
| Edge transition (R19) | all eligible interval incidences | **Yes** — joint product requires both banks to complete |
| Aperture (R18) | none — `D` (direct) or forced `T` (tail) | No — trace failure surfaces later as bank reject |
| Wing tail (R15) | all wedge-valid pairs, least selected | No — selection ignores completion; alternatives are discarded |

Slot 29 realized "resolved terminal, dead trace" twice (R18 at an aperture
with non-candidate `D`; R19 at a transition with candidate `E`). The same
configuration at an aperture whose `D` *is* a candidate, or at a wing whose
least wedge-valid pair is untraceable while a later wedge-valid pair would
trace, is currently a bare seed reject with no recovery clause. Given the
R16→R18→R19 progression, these two classes are the natural predictions for
R20/R21 if their occupancy is nonzero. They are cheap census quantities
(Scan-3).

Semantic remainder (mechanism (b)): a trace that completes with the
first-passing Moore successor is deterministic, but "first-passing equals
design-intended shore" is an unverifiable-by-gate design property. Branch
occupancy (observed: one multi-reachable branch raw, zero after fill, Slot
29/max-u64) should be logged by the census and the branching sites reviewed
once, not scanned repeatedly.

## 4. The trigger universe is finite — why T2 is finishable

Every mechanism-(a) correction requires a *wanted* seed realizing a rejected
or undecided configuration: R16 from fixed extreme slot 19, R18 from C1 slots
29/30, R19 from slot 29. Unwanted seeds that reject are working as designed
and generate no correction.

The wanted universe is finite and closed: seed 0, max-u64, the corpus slots,
and the 4,096-candidate pool (the pool is a fixed candidate set, not "all
seeds"). Call it `W`; |W| ≈ 4,130.

Therefore: computing, for every seed in `W`, each family's *projection* — the
decision-class counts of §3, none of which needs Face/Whole evaluation —
yields the **exhaustive** occupancy of every decided and rejected class over
every seed that could ever trigger a correction. This is not sampling and
needs no saturation heuristic. Combined with §3's totality result, the output
is literally the complete future R-list for mechanisms (a) and (paper-)
undecided, up to mechanism (b):

- every REJECTED class with zero occupancy over `W` can never fire;
- every REJECTED class with nonzero occupancy is a correction found *before*
  freezing, the R15 way;
- U1/U2/O1 are closed by three sentences at the next legitimate reopen.

The 2–4-day estimate in the handover priced "all seeds × all classes ×
full Whole". The census replaces it because the projections are
per-family-local: they need per-record R7 rasters, water predicates in fixed
boxes, bounded traces — not the 3 × 10⁷-column ownership exhaustion that
dominates Whole cost.

## 5. Question 4 — targeted scans, in structural units

All scans are R15-style: structural search over `W`, no promotion, no policy
edit, results are counts keyed by (site, decision class, seed). Costs are
stated as sites × per-site work × seeds; no wall-clock.

**Scan-1 — interval / attachment / junction-pair census (F1, F7, F8).**
Per seed: R7-compile the 61 edge records + 2 perimeters (bounded: ≤
`max_displacement+1` = ≤ 97 reraster probes per record, once each), then
classify each edge station against final planned water. Sites: 61 edges,
~10⁵ stations total per seed; predicate cost per station is a constant-size
capsule/wing/notch test. Add-ons at negligible cost: 102 junction-pair checks,
8 attachment Chebyshev distances, notch-fill counts per bay. Seed-independent
prefilter first: discharge forever every ordinary edge whose envelope cannot
reach water (§3-F1) — expected to cut the 61 edges to roughly the 6 transition
edges plus a handful of bay-adjacent neighbours. Structural cost: ~10⁵
predicate evaluations × |W| on the reduced edge set. Output: interval-count
histogram per edge (any ordinary ≥ 2 or transition-qualifying ≠ 1 is a
finding), attachment distance histogram, junction-pair pass rate, fill-count
distribution.

**Scan-2 — R19 tuple census (F2).** Consumes Scan-1's selected intervals.
Per seed: 8 endpoints × (eligible incidences ≈ interval length ≈ 1.6 × 10³) ×

## 6. Question 5 — freeze staging

### 6.1 The computation is already acyclic

Data-flow stages, each consuming only earlier outputs:

```
F0  catalog structural invariants (rosters, chain shape, far-terminal rule)
S1  per-record R7 displacement: noise → local clip → ceiling C → reraster
    (includes closure, junction departures, perimeters)      [needs F0]
S2  raw bay water: capsules + width jitter + wings, footprint-clipped [S1]
S3  notch fill → final planned water                          [S2]
S4  perimeter classes: aperture intervals, spans, attachments [S1, base predicate]
S5  terminal resolutions: aperture D/T, wing K + tail pairs   [S3, S4]
S6  R18 intervals + attachment endpoints                      [S1, S3, S4]
S7  R19 joint tuples → final edges                            [S5, S6, trace rules]
S8  bank materialization                                      [S5, S7, S3]
S9  faces + Whole                                             [all]
S10 relief fields                                             [S7 edges, S1 junctions]
SEL extreme-selector scalars                                  [S1 only]
```

No cycle exists; the handover's circularity is a *process* property: the
Source freezes as one unit, an independent compiler reproduces it, a mismatch
at any stage reopens everything.

### 6.2 The proposed acyclic staging

1. **Freeze per stage, in DAG order, with per-stage checksums.** A stage
   freezes when (i) its §3 decision table is total (done, minus U1/U2/O1),
   (ii) its census over `W` shows every occupied class is DECIDED — no
   occupied REJECTED class — and (iii) the compiler reproduces that stage's
   projections on the witness seeds. A correction at stage k then reopens
   stages ≥ k only, instead of the whole Source.
2. **Scope the pool pin to what the pool reads.** The selector consumes S1
   scalars only — by design, and every correction R16–R19 states "changes no
   scalar identity or value". Yet the pool authority pins the complete Source
   and compiler bytes, so every post-S1 correction has invalidated the
   measured pool (the single most expensive recurring cost in the R-series).
   Pinning the pool to an S1-scope authority hash (R7 records + displacement
   policy + T1 arithmetic) makes the pool and ranking survive all geometry
   corrections. This is a process/pinning change, not a Source semantic
   change; it needs the user's explicit decision, consistent with the
   existing "pool runs only on explicit GO" rule.
   **Implemented 2026-08-15** as `tools/wp40/t2_s1_authority.lua`, commits
   `85c4a71` and `291de57`; the pool was re-measured under it on 2026-08-16
   (`527b3a5`). One detail
   of the proposal above was not adopted and must not be: the displacement
   *policy* prose is deliberately excluded from the pin, because every one of
   R16--R19 rewrote it while changing no geometric input. Adding it back would
   change `s1_authority_sha256` and fail the gate closed against a valid pool.
3. **Order of operations that this enables:** freeze S1 → measure the pool
   once → winners are fixed → run censuses (Scans 1–3) over `W` → close any
   occupied reject class *before* freezing S2–S8 → freeze S2–S8 staged →
   Scan-4 on the structured subset → S9 freeze.

### 6.3 The minimum feedback edge, and why it is irreducible

Two design axioms: (A) winners are selected on pre-displacement scalars only
("seed selection never depends on the compiled result it selects"); (B) a
geometry failure on a selected seed rejects without a fallback seed, and a
non-compilable corpus is unacceptable (R16's recorded rationale).

Under (A), winner compilability cannot be selected for; under (B), a winner
realizing any rejected configuration forces a policy amendment. Therefore the
edge "winner geometry evaluation → policy" cannot be removed by any ordering —
it is the irreducible feedback edge, and it is exactly one edge: from the S9
verification of the finitely many wanted seeds back into whichever stage's
reject class fired.

What CAN be done is to make that edge fire zero times: the edge is dead if
and only if no seed in `W` occupies any REJECTED class — precisely what the
§5 censuses establish or refute, exhaustively, before any freeze. With zero
occupancy the staging in 6.2 is fully acyclic in practice; with nonzero
occupancy each occupied class is a correction found on paper+census rather
than by a day-long reproduce-diagnose-refreeze cycle. Either way the schedule
is bounded: the correction list is the occupied-class list, and `W` is fixed.

## 7. What could not be bounded, plainly

1. **F10/F11 cause space (mechanism (b)).** A wrongness that preserves every
   current invariant is invisible to enumeration and to every scan. Bounded
   only by a one-time review of the invariant list against design intent
   (§3-F10). This is the honest limit of the method.
2. **Bank-trace semantic correctness.** The trace rules are total and
   deterministic, but "the deterministic result is the design-intended
   shore" at branch sites is a design judgment; the census can log branch
   sites (rare — observed 0–1 per trace) for one review, not decide them.
3. **Everything else was bounded.** Nine of eleven families are closed on
   paper (§3) with two text ambiguities (U1, U2) and one proof obligation
   (O1) as the complete undecided set; the remaining risk is enumerated as
   explicit reject classes whose occupancy over the finite wanted universe
   the §5 censuses measure exhaustively. The predicted next corrections, if
   their occupancy is nonzero, are the two completion-asymmetry classes:
   aperture-anchor-dead with candidate `D`, and wing-pair-dead with a live
   wedge-valid alternative (§3-F3).
