# Round 13 next playtest

Use a fresh world. Gameplay GUI acceptance is separate from the passed static,
focused LuaJIT and disposable native callback/persistence checks.

1. **Start and tools:** each class starts with its Bronze weapon immediately
   usable at level 1. Scout also receives a Bronze Sword and 200 arrows. Basics
   immediately shows Wood/Stone tools including Stone Hoe. Woodcutting Axes are
   tools, cannot enter Weapon and do no combat damage. Check wear outside Creative.
2. **Personal city workspaces:** place different ingredients in two city stations;
   close/reopen and reconnect/restart. Contents remain specific to you and that
   physical station. A second player must see their own contents.
3. **Shared stations:** at a player-placed Forge, two players see the same inputs.
   Only the qualified player sees a result. Try ordinary drag, partial take,
   shift transfer, full inventory, and returning a result to an empty input slot.
   Ingredients and profession progress must be spent/granted once.
4. **Preparation and finishing:** a Cook prepares a raw dish; an Alchemist makes
   a mixture in the personal inventory grid. Another character can bake/brew it.
   Only the preparation grants current-tier profession progress. Meat/Fish/Bread
   roasting is universal Basics. Leave a personal furnace while processing and
   return; newly inserted ingredients must not receive the prior idle time.
5. **Named enchants:** choose a legal T1 suffix first, add a prefix, then replace
   one channel. Check exact preview, cost, fixed tier value and preserved wear.
   T1 can enchant a higher-tier item without growing stronger; an enchant above
   target tier is refused. Identical reapplication and the same stat in both
   channels must be refused without cost.
6. **Jewelry:** Goldsmith base trinkets have their identity special and empty
   enchant channels. Choose a primary-stat prefix and HP/Mana/Crit suffix; replace
   either while preserving the special and other channel. No crafting roll is random.
7. **Wear/repair:** outgoing combat or effective in-combat healing wears only the
   main weapon. Incoming settled damage wears one intact armor/offhand item.
   Fully absorbed hits do not count. A broken quiver keeps arrows retrievable but
   stops automatic ammunition/refill. Repair a partly worn and a broken Swift
   weapon; both must retain their enchanted attack speed.
8. **Protection sanity:** compare the same tank equipment with and without the
   deep Protection/Bulwark Unbroken capstone. Higher-level enemies reduce the
   armor percentage; surplus raw armor remains useful. The universal cap is 70%.

Tool lifetime: Wood 30, Stone 60, Bronze 300, Iron 600, Steel 1000,
Silversteel 1500, Embersteel 2000, Abyssal Steel 3000 ordinary successful uses.
Combat lifetime: T1–T6 1000/1500/2000/2500/3000/4000 qualifying actions.
