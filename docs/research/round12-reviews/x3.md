## Findings

### High — H1: Recompense, Word of Ruin and Whitehot bypass accepted-hit settlement

Locations: `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/kits.lua:817–821`, `:1097–1109`,
`:543–549`; `mods/CORE/grug_core/combat.lua:1126–1130`, `:1189`.

Recompense adds its absorb unconditionally after calling damage, including
when damage returns zero (dodge/friendly refusal). Word of Ruin interprets
the published damage return as acceptance, although `deal_ability_damage`
returns the amount after a successfully called `punch` even when a mob hook
or PvP/absorb pipeline refuses all actual damage. Whitehot's new `on_crit`
callback runs before `target:punch`, so a refused critical attempt starts its
window and consumes its 120-second ICD. Last Word also starts its window/ICD
before damage acceptance.

Reproduction: dodge a talented Smite and observe a fresh Recompense shield;
cast Word of Ruin against a cancelling mob or fully absorbing PvP opponent
and receive drain healing; force a Fireball crit against a cancelling mob
and observe Whitehot. The independent LuaJIT diagnostic directly reproduced
Recompense producing an absorb when the damage seam returns zero.

Fix: carry the exact attacker/target/action and rolled critical/published
amount through the existing accepted-mob/HP-loss settlement authority; commit
these effects once only from acceptance. A positive published return alone
is insufficient. Preserve Word of Ruin's specified pre-armor amount once an
accepted hit is established.

### High — H2: Turn Aside is unreachable with the real talent model

Locations: `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/kits.lua:917–926`;
`mods/PLAYER/grug_classes/talents.lua:634–639`, `:983–991`;
`mods/PLAYER/grug_classes/stats.lua:130–133`.

Turn Aside is registered `window = true`. Its bonus accessor returns zero
until its window starts, but the cast asks that accessor whether it should
start a separate status, and never starts the talent window. Consequently
even rank 3 grants no dodge to any shield target. The behavior fixture hides
this by returning the desired bonus unconditionally from a mock.

If the initial gate alone is fixed, the status still lasts its entire timer
after Power Word: Shield is consumed, and a subsequent untalented shield
refresh does not remove it. If a talent window is added naïvely, self-casts
can also count both the talent bonus and status modifier in stats.

Fix: resolve the ranked magnitude without requiring an already-active window,
attach it to the actual target's named Power Word: Shield contribution, and
end it on that contribution's consumption/replacement/expiry and applicable
lifecycle cleanup. Count it once under the ordinary dodge cap. Test with the
real talent accessor, including casting on another class.

### High — H3: Broadstroke and Recompense wear equipment more than once per action

Locations: `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/kits.lua:405–412`, `:817–821`;
`mods/CORE/grug_core/combat.lua:1136`;
`mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/init.lua:1261`, `:1291`.

Each Broadstroke secondary calls damage without the primary swing's action
identity, causing `deal_ability_damage` to create a fresh identity per target.
The primary already settled its own identity. Recompense similarly uses an
implicit identity for Smite damage and a different `{}` for the absorb.
The repair subscriber deduplicates by identity, so a three-target cleave
wears equipped gear three times; a landed Smite plus Recompense wears twice.

Fix: pass the authoritative primary swing identity into the post-effect and
reuse it for all cleave damage; create one Smite identity and share it with
its accepted Recompense effect. The independent diagnostic confirms that both
secondary-hit calls omit an identity. Test actual repair settlement counts,
including a refused secondary target.

### High — H4: Cinderfall cannot burst on ground, walls or other first contacts

Location: `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/kits.lua:1054–1057`.

The decided ability bursts at the first thing the crosshair ray meets, but
the implementation requires `current_enemy_target`, rejecting every node,
friendly object, and other blocking contact. A Mage cannot aim at ground
between enemies; on a hostile object it centers on `target:get_pos()` rather
than the actual first ray intersection.

Fix: consume the shared structured combat ray directly, retain its first
physical intersection, and damage only hostiles in the decided radius about
that point. Test node, object, friendly/blocker, empty-ray and range cases
without reading enemy memory.

### Medium — M1: Brand increases radius instead of keeping the decided 2 m

Locations: `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/kits.lua:550–552`, `:623–626`.

`fireball_splash = {2,3,4}` is the per-rank damage addition, but it is also
passed to `get_objects_inside_radius` as the radius. Rank 2 hits at 3 m and
rank 3 at 4 m, versus the fixed 2 m in §2.3. The independent LuaJIT probe
reproduced rank 3 requesting 4 m.

Fix: keep radius 2 and separately assemble the ranked damage plus half spell
power. Assert inside/outside spatial boundaries at every rank.

### Medium — M2: Recompense never pays its decided 6% mana cost

Locations: `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/kits.lua:803`;
`mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/init.lua:155–167`.

Smite remains `{mana_percent = 5}` and the effective-cost seam handles only
Whitehot. A Recompense Priest therefore receives the replacement benefit at
the untalented price. This was also observed in the diagnostic.

Fix: resolve Smite to 6% while Recompense is ranked, before affordability,
and spend that same snapshot once. Check exact rounding with non-divisible
base pools, insufficient mana and untalented 5% behavior.

### Medium — M3: New hostile spell branches ignore timed spell-damage bonuses

Locations: `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/kits.lua:625–626`, `:682–684`,
`:1058–1066`, `:1101–1105`.

Brand splash, Rimebite, Cinderfall and Word of Ruin pass raw formulas directly
to damage without `spell_damage_value`. Existing Fireball and Smite use that
helper because the central damage seam applies level scaling, not the timed
spell percentage. With an active spell-damage potion/status these new spells
receive no increase, violating `combat_stats.md` §2's multiplier on every
fully assembled hostile spell formula.

Fix: apply the shared spell multiplier once to each complete raw formula,
with an explicit consistent snapshot point for projectile splash. Verify
that support/shield amounts do not acquire this hostile-damage multiplier.

### Medium — M4: Hold Ground immunity survives respec

Locations: `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/kits.lua:1041`;
`mods/PLAYER/grug_classes/talents.lua:1117–1121`;
`mods/CORE/grug_core/movement.lua:350–360`.

Hold Ground writes only the movement aggregator's immunity timer. Respec
clears the talent-window table and refreshes stats/kit, with no call clearing
that immunity. Casting Hold Ground then respeccing leaves root/slow immunity
until its old eight-second deadline. §3.2/§3.7 explicitly require timed
talent windows, including Hold Ground immunity, to clear on respec.

Fix: add bounded ownership-aware lifecycle cleanup for this immunity, while
preserving unrelated Scout/movement state. Test the real movement aggregator
through respec/admin level reset as well as death and leave.

### Medium — M5: Last Light shares the Power Word: Shield contribution name

Locations: `mods/CORE/grug_core/combat.lua:1323–1325`;
`mods/PLAYER/grug_trinkets/init.lua:130`.

The compatibility-shaped `set_absorb` wrapper assigns every caller the source
name `power_word_shield`, but the shipped Last Light trinket also calls it.
A Priest shield therefore replaces Last Light's remaining 120-second absorb,
and a Last Light trigger replaces the Priest's contribution. This undermines
the new independent named-contribution aggregator and would also corrupt
Turn Aside ownership once fixed.

Fix: move every shipped writer to an explicit source identity, including
Last Light, retaining its shared cooldown and maximum lifetime. Test coexistence,
same-source refresh, total cap and earliest-expiry-first consumption.
