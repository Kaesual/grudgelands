# Round 12 visual gallery

The plates show actual inventory sprites at native size and nearest-neighbour
magnification. Each pair is **before → after**. Accepted swords, one-handed
axes, armor and harvested crop icons remain unchanged.

## Cooked foods and preparations

[Native-size catalog](../../tools/r12_art/evidence/food-before-after-native.png)
· [Enlarged comparison](../../tools/r12_art/evidence/food-before-after-6x.png)

Twenty-seven entries include the eighteen dishes, six uncooked preparations,
Bread, retained Cooked Meat and retained Cooked Fish. Jungle Cocoa now reads as
a brown hot drink; preparations and finished meals have separate silhouettes.
