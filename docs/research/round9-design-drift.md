# Round 9 design-doc drift audit (R9-AUDIT, GPT-5.6 Sol, read-only, 2026-09-19)

Read-only audit of the design docs against the rulings of rounds 5–9 and the shipped code (main 71ed60df). Report only; the user rules per item, R9-DOCS applies the doc changes. Findings that touch a running lane were forwarded to that lane's orchestrator thread.

## Summary

- 20 relevant deviations: 17 require the specification to be brought in line with later decisions; 3 need a new/clarifying ruling before implementation.
- The drift is concentrated in running R9 Lanes: MAP-C, PROF-A/B, MOB2, BOSS, UI, CAP and MAP-B (`TODO-round9.md:55-352`).
- Five deviations are already visible today: station craftability, assigned boss tier, capital size, mana floor and the outdated food summary.
- The R5/R6 pool and mob formulas as well as the R7 level bands and food tables otherwise match the code (`docs/design/combat_stats.md:22-24,393-404,582-629`; `mods/CORE/grug_core/combat.lua:44-50`; `mods/ENTITIES/grug_mobs/levels.lua:70-118`; `mods/ITEMS/grug_food/init.lua:6-44`).
- The V1/V2 boundary is correct in ROADMAP and the world specification; only `story.md` still describes V1 Nether portals (`ROADMAP.md:93-95,308-310`; `docs/design/world.md:640-642`; `docs/design/story.md:22-24,52-65`).

## Findings

1. **[R9-MAP-C] ruling 9 (round 9, `TODO-round9.md:105-110,451-453`) vs. `world_zones.md` §13.1 (`docs/design/world_zones.md:1352-1364`).** The specification records all six previous terrain/climate NoiseParams as the authenticated baseline; the running code still has nonconstant terrain noise and `caverns,mountains,ridges` (`mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r7_native.lua:226-255`; `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/mapgen_manifest.lua:44-46`). R9 sets both terrain NoiseParams to the same plateau and removes Mountains/Ridges. **Classification: doc follows the decision.** Exact replacement:

   > At the R9 cutover, `mgv7_np_terrain_base` and `mgv7_np_terrain_alt` are identical constant NoiseParams with `scale = 0` and `offset = PLATEAU_Y`; the four climate NoiseParams remain authenticated inputs. `mgv7_spflags = caverns,nofloatlands`, and the canonical enabled-feature token is `caverns`. Native v7 remains the cave, ore, dungeon and stratum substrate below the authored surface, not the surface-height authority.

2. **[R9-MAP-C] ruling 9 (round 9, `TODO-round9.md:111-137,451-453`) vs. `world_zones.md` §7.6 (`docs/design/world_zones.md:605-647`).** The specification still describes the R8 writer with 80-node cells, candidate gate, lumen and flood-fill proof; this writer is enabled by default in the code (`mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/r6_settlement.lua:637-641`). R9 disables it and replaces it with the native surface skin. The README also still names “connected cave mouths” as running behavior (`README.md:272-276`). **Classification: doc follows the decision.** Exact replacement:

   > **Native-cave surface skin (2026-09-19):** For final authored height `T`, the band `T−3..T−1` replaces preserved immutable native air with host rock. A column opens only when the native-air run from `T−1` downward is at least four nodes and either one cardinal neighbour is at least two nodes lower or all nine columns of the surrounding 3×3 meet the same air-run test. An opening omits the surface node and the skin; water-bed columns receive the skin and never open. The band is clipped at y = −37 and never applies inside starts, capitals, settlements, POIs, landmarks, routes/corridors, functional surfaces, foundations, housing or static exclusions. The R8 authored cave-mouth writer is disabled and its candidate/count evidence is historical.

3. **[R9-MAP-C] ruling 10 (round 9, `TODO-round9.md:138-171,454-457`) vs. `world_zones.md` §7.4 (`docs/design/world_zones.md:479-505`).** The R8 specification freezes profile shares of 40/25/20/15, 4–10 nodes of sand and four nodes of transition. R9 requires 20–28 nodes of beach, 16–24 nodes of blend, no upper slope limit, relief-dependent shares without fixed percentages, and no sand on mountain coasts or mountain lakes. **Classification: doc follows the decision.** Exact replacement:

   > **R9 coast band:** Each eligible 48-node shore run has a 20–28-node beach rising one node per 2–5 nodes, followed by a 16–24-node linear blend into the owning relief; this is a shape rule, not a slope cap. Wetland and lowland are mostly beach, rolling relief has a medium share, plateau/highland little, and mountain relief none; exact shares are not fixed, but beaches must recur. Mountain coasts and mountain lakes use stone, gravel, bluff, cliff or terrace fronts and never sand. Freshwater rims are 2–4 nodes of sand in wetland/lowland/rolling relief and stone or gravel in plateau/highland/mountain relief.

4. **[R9-PROF-A/B] ruling 2 (round 9, `TODO-round9.md:227-251,429-431`) vs. `items_crafting.md` §0/§3 and `inventory_equipment.md` §4 (`docs/design/items_crafting.md:64-66,340-343`; `docs/design/inventory_equipment.md:255-259`).** The general rule calls profession stations uncraftable and recognizes only Grid/Furnace/Dual Furnace/Brewing Stand. This is already incorrect for the craftable T3 Brewing Stand (`mods/ITEMS/grug_alchemy/recipes.lua:145-157`), and R9 generalizes this rule to all six primary professions. The current whitelist contains only four stations (`mods/PLAYER/grug_jobs/registry.lua:20-25`). **Classification: doc follows the decision.** Exact replacement:

   > Vendor supplies remain a small gold sink. Every primary profession has one public station beside its trainer in every capital. The station ids are `brewing_stand`, `forge`, `tanning_rack`, `tailor_bench`, `carving_bench` and `jewellers_bench`, in addition to `grid`, `furnace` and `dual_furnace`. Every profession station item is craftable for housing through a non-circular T3 grid recipe of its owning profession; a station is never crafted at itself.

Broken Causeway/Shattered Line add the 24-hour elite War Construct, level-21–50 savanna adds the daytime Speargrass Tiger, and beach/coast rows add Shore Crab and its level-45–60 elite Reef Lurker variant.

7. **[R9-BOSS] ruling 13/14 (round 9, `TODO-round9.md:263-293,298-306,462-468`) vs. `world.md` §4b and `biomes_mobs.md` §3.1 (`docs/design/world.md:593-613`; `docs/design/biomes_mobs.md:1159-1165`).** The design and current code describe stationary three-position dragons with Breath Line and Ground Slam; the code sets speed to zero (`mods/ENTITIES/grug_mobs/bosses.lua:302-323`). R9 decides ground/flight movement, Dive Slam, Cone Breath with ground effect, Lightning, Gust and Enrage with at most two Whelps. **Classification: doc follows the decision.** Exact replacement:

   > **V1 dragon chassis:** Both dragons are mobile ground-and-flight bosses. They may take off, pursue and settle within their lair leash; Dive Slam snapshots its target after a one-second telegraph. Cone Breath fires three projectiles at −15°/0°/+15° and leaves temporary rime or scorch; Stormscale also uses a 1.5-second telegraphed radius-2 lightning strike. Wing Gust knocks back and slows nearby hostile players. At 50% HP the dragon enrages, shortens its cooldowns and summons at most two clearly smaller, weaker, skill-less whelps. Particle effects are bounded to hundreds, never thousands, and no boss HUD is added.

10. **[R9-UI] rulings 17/19 (round 9, `TODO-round9.md:316-334,344-348,472-479`) vs. `items_crafting.md` §2.2/§3.6 and `inventory_equipment.md` §4 (`docs/design/items_crafting.md:284-300,981-986`; `docs/design/inventory_equipment.md:251-259`).** The documents specify only list rows and Book buttons. The current code renders a paginated row list and ends with `button_exit`; trainers are generically named “Trainer,” and the Brewing Stand uses a simple custom nodebox look (`mods/PLAYER/grug_jobs/ui.lua:149-203`; `mods/ENTITIES/grug_mobs/start_villagers.lua:386`; `mods/ITEMS/grug_brewing/node.lua:310-316`). **Classification: doc follows the decision.** Exact addition:

Close returns to `sfinv:crafting`. Trainer names derive from the registered profession rather than the generic Trainer label. The Brewing Stand uses the licensed VoxeLibre visual treatment with an independently authored nodebox; its two-reagent, vial, fuel and output mechanics remain unchanged.

11. **[R9-UI] ruling 18 (round 9, `TODO-round9.md:335-343,474-477`) vs. `items_crafting.md` §2/§2.2/D4 (`docs/design/items_crafting.md:211-216,284-305,2490-2495`).** The design currently requires the complete T1–T6 catalog with higher tiers greyed out; the code provides all profession entries and greys them out only by tier (`mods/PLAYER/grug_jobs/ui.lua:104-123,149-198`). R9 makes visibility depend on unlocked tier and ingredients having been seen once, without changing craft permission. The root README and Current State also still say “complete UI books” (`README.md:137-147,298-301`). **Classification: doc follows the decision.** Exact replacement:

   > A learned profession exposes only recipes whose tier is currently unlocked and whose every input token has been seen in the player’s `main` or `craft` inventory; seeing any matching item satisfies a `group:…` token. Undiscovered recipes are counted per tier but not listed or searchable. Craft permission remains profession-tier-only and never depends on discovery. Unlearning wipes profession level and current-tier progress but preserves the player’s seen-item set. General engine recipes remain in the separate always-open General book.

12. **[R9-UI] ruling 18 has an internal conflict (`TODO-round9.md:335-343` vs. `TODO-round9.md:474-477`).** The Lane text says that learning a profession marks its T1 recipes as seen; the summarizing user ruling, by contrast, requires “tier unlocked AND every ingredient held once” for every listed recipe. The underlying persistence model stores seen item names, not recipe IDs (`TODO-round9.md:335-340`). **Classification: decision violates the doc and needs a ruling.** Question: **Are T1 recipes automatically visible upon learning, or must all ingredients have been held once for T1 as well?** If automatically: Should all T1 input tokens be marked as seen, or should T1 receive an explicit exception from the ingredient test?

15. **R9-MOUNTS ruling 5 (round 9, `TODO-round9.md:381-395,438-441`) vs. `mounts.md` §1.1/§3 (`docs/design/mounts.md:36-48,102-128`).** The document says that each purchased animal persists as its own item and is never removed; R9 replaces T1 with T2 and T3 with T4 and defines the models. **Classification: doc follows the decision.** Exact replacement:

   > Riding ownership remains permanent player state, but inventory representation is one item per movement mode: buying T2 atomically replaces the T1 land-mount item, and buying T4 atomically replaces the T3 flying-mount item. Restoration recreates only the highest owned land and flying representations. T1 is a faction-coloured horse; T2 is Human horse, Dwarf ibex/ram, Elf stag, Orc boar, Undead wolf or skeletal-horse retint, and Troll tiger; T3/T4 are Accord eagle/Steller’s sea eagle and Throng cave bat/giant bat, with the higher tier using the nobler colour treatment. Dragons are never mounts.

19. **R8 orchestrator ruling on mana (round 8, `TODO-round8.md:98-104`) vs. `combat_stats.md` §5 and shipped code (`docs/design/combat_stats.md:796-801`; `mods/PLAYER/grug_abilities/init.lua:124-136`).** The user approved Option A from the Cooking/Alchemy plan: `max(¼ OOC, 0.25% max mana/s)` (`docs/research/cooking-alchemy-plan.md:85-114`).
