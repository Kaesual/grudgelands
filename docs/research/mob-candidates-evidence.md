The inventory follows every literal mob-registration call and expands two
registration loops: the twelve `goblins` subtype records passed through
`goblins.generate` (`goblins/init.lua:159-168`,
`goblins/utilities.lua:65-88`) and the five fire plus five ice dragon-egg
entities produced by the two `draconis` loops
(`draconis/craftitems.lua:131-274,278-421`). Projectile, spawn-egg item, lasso,
and visual-helper entity registrations are not mob definitions. Media lists
include the filenames directly referenced by a definition and inherited base
table; sound stems were expanded to the matching OGG files in that project's
sound tree. “Static/no animation table” means exactly that no named animation
range is supplied by the mob definition; it is not a binary-chunk claim about
the mesh.

Kinds report movement and attack facts together, so a row can be both
`flying` and `ranged`. “Boss” is used only where the source definition is an
explicit major boss (the two full dragons, Ender Dragon, Wither, and Elder
Guardian), not merely because a name contains “wither”. Approximate size is
the registered collision/hit box and `visual_size`, without converting from a
source framework's scale convention.
