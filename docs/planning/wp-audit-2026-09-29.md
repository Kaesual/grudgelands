partial work package, and every BACKLOG item outside the WP table, against

audit. It is the basis for cleaning up [BACKLOG](../../BACKLOG.md) and

packages that accompany the organic capital-wall round (Round 26).

Classes: **valid**, **partly done**, **stale** (the intent still holds, the text
does not), **wrong** (contradicts a later ruling), **superseded / merge**,
**needs user decision**. Size is the remaining work (S/M/L). *Wall fit* says
whether the remainder touches capital mapgen (**conflict**), helps a capital
outline change (**synergy**) or neither (**independent**). The evidence (file:line)
is in the lane reports in the appendix.

## User decisions (2026-09-29)

Answered on the decision page; notes in the user's words are paraphrased.

**A. Wall round.** A1 stay inside today's 512 square for now; the user wants
2D top-down images first and may revise. A2 star-shaped outline, only more
irregular. A3 "smaller, denser" is retired as a rule, but the size stays
roughly as today: the goal is flair and a size that fits the content, with
more irregular outlines and a few well-placed style elements, no pointless
filling. A4 include the D72 "capitals look alike / infill" follow-up. A5 the
waypoint pad may be anywhere in the city.

**B. V1 scope.** B1 boats are V1. B2 waypoints are V1, starts and capitals
only. B3 `/unstuck` is dropped (travel home suffices). B4 WP41 geographic PvP
is V1. B5 WP42 scripted NPC battles are post-V1; PvP POIs (small forts and
camps with NPCs) may come in V1. B6 WP42 may precede WP41. B7 story levels
41–60 and the finale are V1.

**C. Close / merge.** C1 close WP27, WP29, WP37, WP49; close WP23 and fold its
remainder into WP17/WP34/WP41. C2 merge WP7, WP11, WP30 and the price parts of
WP22/WP31 into WP44. C3 WP31 delivered. C4 WP32 delivered, GUI acceptance
pending. C5 WP14 carried light canceled (WP14 closes as delivered). C6 the two
surface critters (Bone Weevil, Bog Fowl) still get ×0.75; the rest of WP37 is
closed. WP21 closed: food-based regeneration is the final recovery system.

**D. Economy and items.** D1 WP44 as a lighter pass. D2 rescale quest copper
onto the ×2.5 axis in the same cutover. D3 keep masterwork: upgrade an existing
item to item level 70. D4 named rares drop no trophies. D5 remove the mobs_redo
utility items (nametag, net, lasso, protector, saddle, repellent, shears). D6
remove the silver-sandstone recipes. D7 repair at stations inside a claim, at
the trader price (convenience only). D8 dig speed stays as in the game; close
WP22's remainder. D9 unify the tool namespace now. D10 status icons become
their own small UI package; the user will have the icons generated from a
complete effect list. D11 cultural-master NPCs later, with WP10.

**E. World.** E1 `bandit_frontier` stays 16 (fix the design). E2 a separate
POI walk is wanted. E3 T6 lava lakes stay planned. E4 Land Guard and Rift Spawn
join the future depth pulse. E5 renewable ores are removed entirely, camps
included; WP34 keeps only the depth pulse (kept, later; its placement and
the servant roster below −1000 are still open). E6 WP46 deferred (no terrain
damage exists today). E7 a future fire/explosion guard also spares roads and
settlement cores. E8 WP48 parked until a problem shows. E9 the PUC run is
optional. E10 no mandatory native smoke: checks with judgement, sometimes
thorough, never rigid rules that force hour-long tests. E11 no dragon hoard
chest. E12 two claims suppressing a rare is acceptable. E13 silver/tin and
basalt lines dropped.

## Capital walls and outlines: current state (for the organic-wall round)

**1. Where the wall line is computed.**
- `mods/MAPGEN/grug_mapgen/wp40/capital_planner.lua`, `M.plan` step 3 (`:529-627`). This runs once per world in main after height, water and roads. `r7_capitals.lua:104-266` calls it; the result is cached per D71.
- The outline is **polar and star-shaped**:
  - 180 rays (`NRAY`, `:61`) from the anchor, each starting at the civic core (`s0 = CORE/m`).
  - Along each ray a cost is accumulated over the 2-node sample grid: wet ×4.0, steep ×2.6, buildable ×1.0.
  - The ray gets a low-frequency simplex "budget noise" factor, 1 ± 0.3 (`:533,551-552`).
  - `outline_for(B)` cuts each ray where the accumulated cost exceeds `B·f` (`:555-565`).
  - Then 4 passes of a 9-ray circular moving average (`SMOOTH_PASSES 4, SMOOTH_W 4`, `:566-574`). This heavy smoothing is why cities read round.
  - A clamp to `[R_MIN 110, rmax]`, where `rmax = min((HALF 256 − BAND 26)/m, 260)` (`:541,575`), then three "keep off banks" passes that push a ray to the nearest point ≥ `WALL_BANK` 6 from water (`:576-597`).
  - The budget `B` is bisected until the polygon area reaches `TARGET_AREA = 100000` m² (`:59,606-613`).
- `r_at(phi)` interpolates between rays, and `inside(lx, lz, margin)` is `r ≤ r_at(φ) − margin` (`:616-626`). Both assume a star shape.
- **Wall polyline:** the ray endpoints are resampled every ~2 nodes into `wall.pts` (`:1244-1263`).
- Then come gatehouse boxes, gaps, the walk profile (≤ ½ per column), arcades over water, turrets every `TURRET_EVERY`, and the civic-lake flags `l`/`f` (`M.shore_flags`, `:233`).
- Edge dimensions per kind are in `M.EDGE` (`:115-127`); `r7_capitals.lua:185` applies them.
- **Payload** (`M.serialize`, `:1931`):
  - `o` holds the 180 radii. It is parsed by `deserialize`, which requires it to be non-empty (`:2048`), but nothing outside the planner reads it (grep `.outline` over `mods/`: no other consumer).
  - `w` holds wall points with walk and flags; `t` holds turrets.
- The writer is `wp13/city_edge.lua`. Per column it projects onto the **nearest polyline segment** (`city_edge.lua:14-24`), so it does not depend on the polar form.

**2. How `capital_protection.lua` derives protection.**
- `M.build(layout, lake)` (`wp40/capital_protection.lua:74-212`) uses **only `layout.wall.pts`**. Per node row it takes the even-odd crossings of the closed polyline and turns them into x-intervals (`:89-107`). The whole civic lake is added as node runs (`:109-129`).
- It then applies an exact Euclidean dilation by `EDGE_REACH 9 + BAND 12 = 21` nodes (`:33,38,132-153`), merged into per-row integer intervals. `member(x, z)` is one table index plus one or two comparisons (`:184-199`).
- It fails the load if the protected shape leaves ±`BOUND_HALF` 266 (`:44,157-160`).
- `r7_runtime.lua:123,323` installs it for every capital.
- Consumers:
  - The hard footprint `hard_capital_city_v1` (`source/simple_map.lua:469`, `bound_width=532`), used as a candidate box in `zones.lua:879-894,927-934`.
  - The claim/vegetation/cave exclusions: the bare band (`simple_map.lua:438-446,495-499,540-553`).
  - Mob spawn refusal (`grug_mobs/spawn_policy.lua:339`).
  - The boot log (`r7_loader.lua:252-275`).
- Because it already handles arbitrary simple polygons (even-odd, multiple intervals per row, `MORE`), **an irregular or non-star wall line needs no change here**, provided the polyline stays closed and does not self-intersect. `EDGE_REACH` assumes compass-aligned gatehouses sliding ≤ ~34° (`:27-33`).

**3. How the planner uses the outline.**
- Gates: four cardinal gates slide along the outline (≤ `GATE_SLIDE` 60, ×2.5 near water, ≤ `GATE_MAX_ANGLE` 0.6 rad) toward the road ends (`:629-720`).
- Streets: ring lanes sit at `RINGS {0.31, 0.71}` fractions between the core gate radius and `r_at` (`:67`). The street guide keeps streets inside (`:897`).
- Plots: each plot must be `inside(…, WALL_HALF + WALL_KEEP)` (`:1656-1658`).
- Connectors: they are pushed outside `r_at + WALL_HALF + 6` except near their gate (`:1850-1858`).
- Placement tiers (Round 25 Lane H): the remaining named-building drops (2 of 200 seeds) are a capacity limit of the area inside the outline (`round25-housing-plan.md:276-280`).
